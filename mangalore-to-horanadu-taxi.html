Mangalore to Horanadu Travel Route

The journey from Mangalore to Horanadu travels through scenic parts of coastal Karnataka and the Western Ghats. The approximate road distance is around 110 to 120 km, depending on the selected route and exact pickup and destination locations.

Travel time is generally around 3 to 4 hours, although traffic, weather, road conditions and sightseeing stops can affect the journey.

Travellers can also plan Horanadu together with Sringeri, Chikkamagalur, Kudremukh, Dharmasthala or Kukke Subramanya for a longer Karnataka temple and sightseeing itinerary.
