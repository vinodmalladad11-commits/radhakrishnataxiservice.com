Mangalore to Sringeri Travel Route

The journey from Mangalore to Sringeri takes you from the coastal region of Karnataka towards the scenic Western Ghats. The approximate road distance is around 105 to 115 km, depending on the route and exact pickup and destination locations.

Travel time is generally around 3 to 4 hours, although traffic, weather, road conditions and sightseeing stops can affect the journey time.

Travellers can also plan Sringeri together with Horanadu, Chikkamagalur, Kudremukh, Dharmasthala or Kukke Subramanya depending on the available travel time.
