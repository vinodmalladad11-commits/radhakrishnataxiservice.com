Explore Karnataka's Sacred Destinations

Karnataka is home to celebrated pilgrimage centres, ancient temple architecture and spiritual destinations set among the Western Ghats and the Arabian Sea coast. A carefully planned temple tour can bring together Shiva, Devi, Vishnu and Subrahmanya temples in a single journey.

Radhakrishna Taxi Service helps you plan temple travel from Mangalore according to your chosen route, group size and number of days. You can focus on a particular temple, visit a pair of nearby pilgrimage centres, or request a longer circuit covering several districts.

Popular Temple Tours in Karnataka

Open each destination to learn about the temple and nearby places. You can request an itinerary based on the locations you want to visit.

1. Dharmasthala Sri Manjunatha Temple

Visit the renowned Sri Manjunatha Swamy Temple, an important pilgrimage destination in Dakshina Kannada. Depending on your schedule, the trip may also include the Bahubali statue at Ratnagiri Hill and the Manjusha Museum, subject to opening hours.

Suggested duration: A day trip or part of a multi-temple tour.

Explore Dharmasthala & Kukke Package
2. Kukke Sri Subrahmanya Temple

Located amid the Western Ghats, Kukke Subrahmanya is a major pilgrimage centre associated with Lord Subrahmanya and serpent worship. Pilgrims planning special sevas or rituals should confirm booking requirements directly with the temple.

Nearby options: Adi Subrahmanya Temple and the Kumaradhara area.

Explore Dharmasthala & Kukke Package
3. Sringeri Sri Sharadamba Temple

Sringeri is a celebrated spiritual centre on the banks of the Tunga River. Visit Sri Sharadamba Temple, Vidyashankara Temple and other nearby religious landmarks while allowing time for temple darshan and a peaceful riverside visit.

Suggested duration: Combine with Horanadu for a multi-day pilgrimage.

Explore Sringeri & Horanadu Package
4. Horanadu Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple

Surrounded by the scenic Western Ghats, Horanadu is known for Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple. The journey can be paired with Sringeri and Kalasa, making it suitable for travellers who want a temple-focused trip through the Malnad region.

Travel tip: Check temple schedules and road conditions before departure.

Explore Sringeri & Horanadu Package
5. Kollur Sri Mookambika Temple

Kollur Sri Mookambika Temple is an important Devi pilgrimage destination near the Western Ghats. It can be included in a route connecting Udupi, Murudeshwara or Sringeri, depending on the available travel time.

Suggested duration: A dedicated temple visit or one stop on a longer circuit.

6. Udupi Sri Krishna Matha

Visit the well-known Sri Krishna Matha in Udupi, a major Vaishnava pilgrimage centre. Depending on the itinerary, you may also visit nearby temples such as Anantheshwara and Chandramouleshwara, while respecting temple schedules.

Nearby sightseeing: Malpe Beach can be added if time permits.

7. Murudeshwara Shiva Temple

Murudeshwara is known for its Shiva temple, monumental Shiva statue, Raja Gopuram and coastal setting. It is a useful stop for pilgrims travelling along the Karnataka coast, with time for temple darshan and the surrounding complex.

Nearby sightseeing: Murudeshwara Beach, subject to your schedule.

Explore Murudeshwara & Gokarna Package
8. Gokarna Mahabaleshwar Temple

Gokarna's Mahabaleshwar Temple is an important Shiva pilgrimage site associated with the Atmalinga tradition. The town also has other religious landmarks and coastal scenery that can be included when time permits.

Suggested combination: Murudeshwara and Gokarna over a longer trip.

Explore Murudeshwara & Gokarna Package
9. Kateel Sri Durgaparameshwari Temple

Kateel is a revered Devi temple situated beside the Nandini River. Its location makes it a possible addition to a Mangalore temple itinerary, particularly when combined with other local shrines on a route planned in advance.

Suggested duration: A half-day visit or a stop on a regional temple circuit.

10. Mangaladevi Temple, Mangalore

Mangaladevi Temple is one of Mangalore's best-known religious landmarks and is associated with the city's name. It can be included in a local temple tour along with other established temples within the city.

Suggested combination: A local Mangalore temple sightseeing trip.

11. Kadri Manjunatha Temple, Mangalore

Kadri Manjunatha Temple is a historic religious site in Mangalore, known for its spiritual significance and traditional architecture. Ask for a local itinerary that includes this temple and other nearby places of worship.

Suggested duration: Part of a half-day or full-day local temple tour.

12. Sri Krishna Temple, Udupi District

Udupi's temple traditions draw pilgrims throughout the year. Visitors can plan a focused darshan visit or combine the destination with nearby religious sites and selected coastal attractions, depending on the time available.

Travel tip: Confirm darshan timings before leaving your hotel or pickup point.

13. Kalaseshwara Temple, Kalasa

Kalasa is a temple town in the Malnad region, commonly paired with Horanadu because of their proximity. The surrounding landscape offers a scenic setting for a relaxed pilgrimage through the Western Ghats.

Suggested combination: Kalasa, Horanadu and Sringeri.

Explore Sringeri & Horanadu Package
14. Anegudde Sri Vinayaka Temple

Anegudde is a well-known Ganesha pilgrimage destination in coastal Karnataka. It can be added to an Udupi and Kundapura area temple itinerary, depending on road travel and darshan time.

Suggested combination: Udupi and other temples in the coastal belt.

15. Thousand Pillar Jain Basadi, Moodbidri

Moodbidri is known for its Jain heritage, including the Thousand Pillar Basadi. It is a worthwhile option for travellers interested in religious heritage, traditional craftsmanship and historic architecture.

Suggested duration: A half-day heritage and temple visit, subject to opening hours.

16. Venur Bahubali and Jain Heritage

Venur is associated with Jain heritage and its Bahubali monument. It can be considered for a heritage-focused route through parts of Dakshina Kannada, alongside other sites selected to suit your trip length.

Travel tip: Confirm access and local visiting arrangements before travelling.

Suggested 4-Day Karnataka Temple Tour

This is a planning example, not a fixed schedule. The final route should be adjusted for your pickup location, temple timings and the time required for darshan.

Day 1

Mangalore and Local Temples

Start with selected Mangalore temples such as Mangaladevi and Kadri Manjunatha. The schedule may be adjusted to your arrival time and the current temple timings.

Day 2

Dharmasthala and Kukke Subrahmanya

Visit Dharmasthala and continue towards Kukke Subrahmanya if darshan queues, road conditions and your selected overnight arrangements allow enough time.

Day 3

Sringeri and Horanadu Region

Travel into the Malnad region to visit Sringeri and Horanadu. This day may require an early start and a planned overnight stay depending on your route.

Day 4

Return Journey or Additional Temple

Return towards your agreed drop-off point or use an additional day for a destination such as Kollur or Murudeshwara. Avoid adding distant stops if they make the journey too rushed.

Important: This itinerary covers geographically spread-out destinations and may be tiring if completed too quickly. For a more relaxed journey, ask us to prepare a route based on your preferred temples and the number of days you have available.
Before you travel

Temple Tour FAQs

Useful information for planning your Karnataka pilgrimage.

Can I customise a temple tour from Mangalore?

Yes. Share the temples you want to visit, your travel dates, passenger count, pickup point and preferred trip duration. We can discuss a route that suits your requirements.

How many days are needed for a Karnataka temple tour?

A focused regional trip may take one or two days. A longer circuit covering Sringeri, Horanadu, Kollur, Udupi, Murudeshwara, Dharmasthala and Kukke Subrahmanya generally needs several days.

Can families and elderly pilgrims book a suitable vehicle?

You can enquire about sedan, SUV and larger group vehicle options based on availability. Tell us the passenger count, luggage requirements and any mobility considerations before booking.

Are temple darshan tickets and special rituals included?

Do not assume that special darshan tickets, sevas, rituals, accommodation, meals or entry fees are included in a taxi quotation. Confirm each item when discussing your trip and check the temple's official information for current arrangements.

What should I check before travelling?

Confirm temple timings, special-event schedules, dress rules, ritual bookings, road conditions and any local restrictions. Keep extra time for queues, meals and rest stops.

How do I get a temple tour quotation?

Contact us by phone or WhatsApp with your travel date, pickup location, destination list, number of passengers and preferred vehicle. The quotation will depend on your selected route, duration and vehicle arrangement.
