Discover the Karnataka Coast

Explore Beaches, Temples and Coastal Heritage

Coastal Karnataka stretches along the Arabian Sea and offers a mix of sandy beaches, fishing harbours, historic religious sites, coastal towns and scenic landscapes. Mangaluru, Udupi and Uttara Kannada each offer distinctive experiences for visitors.

Start with beaches near Mangaluru, continue towards Udupi and Malpe, and explore Kaup Lighthouse or Maravanthe. Travellers with more time can extend their journey to Murudeshwara, Gokarna and the northern Karnataka coast.

Coastal Karnataka Tour FAQs

Which destinations are included in Coastal Karnataka?

Coastal Karnataka includes destinations around Mangaluru, Udupi, Kundapura and Uttara Kannada. Popular stops include Panambur, Tannirbhavi, Malpe, Kaup, Maravanthe, Murudeshwara and Gokarna.

Can I visit all these places in one day?

No. The destinations span a long stretch of coastline. A local Mangaluru tour or a selected Udupi-area itinerary may fit into a day, while Murudeshwara and Gokarna generally need more time. A multi-day plan is preferable for an extended coastal journey.

Can I visit St. Mary's Island throughout the year?

Boat access depends on the season, weather and local operating decisions. Services may be suspended during the monsoon or when sea conditions are unsafe. Confirm ferry operations before including the island in your plan.

When is a good time for a Coastal Karnataka tour?

The cooler months are often comfortable for coastal sightseeing. The monsoon can bring heavy rainfall, rough seas and activity restrictions. Check current weather and local safety guidance before travelling.
