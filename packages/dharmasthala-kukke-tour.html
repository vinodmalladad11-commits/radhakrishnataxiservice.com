Explore Dharmasthala and Kukke Subrahmanya

Discover sacred temples, spiritual landmarks and beautiful surroundings in the Western Ghats region of Karnataka.

Dharmasthala and Kukke Subrahmanya are important pilgrimage destinations in Dakshina Kannada district. Many families and devotees plan visits to both places as part of the same trip. The journey can be organised from Mangalore or another agreed pickup point.

Radhakrishna Taxi Service can help you plan a private taxi journey based on your group size, travel dates and preferred sightseeing stops. You can choose a temple-focused itinerary or include selected nearby attractions if time permits.

Dharmasthala

Sri Manjunatha Swamy Temple

The principal pilgrimage destination at Dharmasthala, known for Lord Manjunatha worship and its long-standing tradition of religious service.

Read more about this temple

The temple is visited by devotees from across Karnataka and beyond. Plan sufficient time for darshan, follow the temple's current dress and visitor guidelines, and check the latest timings before travelling. Darshan queues can vary significantly on weekends, holidays and special occasions.

Dharmasthala

Bahubali Statue and Ratnagiri Hill

A well-known landmark near Dharmasthala featuring the large monolithic statue of Bahubali on Ratnagiri Hill.

Read more about this attraction

The statue is associated with Jain heritage and offers a peaceful place for visitors interested in history, architecture and religious traditions. Allow time for the approach and steps, and check access arrangements locally, especially when travelling with senior citizens.

Dharmasthala

Manjusha Museum

A museum associated with Dharmasthala, known for collections of cultural objects, historical items and heritage exhibits.

Read more about the museum

The museum can add a cultural and historical dimension to your temple visit. Opening hours, visitor access and available exhibits may change, so confirm the current details locally before including it in a fixed schedule.

Dharmasthala

Nethravathi River Bathing Area

A river area traditionally visited by pilgrims travelling to Dharmasthala.

Read more about the river area

Some visitors include the river area as part of their pilgrimage routine. River conditions can change with rainfall and water flow. Follow local safety instructions and use only officially permitted bathing areas.

Kukke Subrahmanya

Sri Kukke Subrahmanya Temple

A prominent temple dedicated to Lord Subrahmanya, situated amid the green landscape of the Western Ghats.

Read more about Kukke Temple

The temple is an important destination for devotees and for families undertaking religious journeys. Rituals, seva bookings, darshan arrangements and waiting times may vary. Check the temple's official information before travel, particularly if you plan to participate in a specific ritual or seva.

Kukke Subrahmanya

Kumaradhara River

A river near Kukke Subrahmanya that forms part of the area's natural and pilgrimage landscape.

Read more about the river

Pilgrims often ask about the river when planning their temple visit. Conditions vary seasonally, especially during heavy monsoon rainfall. Follow local guidance, avoid unsafe currents and do not enter restricted sections.

Kukke Subrahmanya

Adi Subrahmanya Temple

A sacred site associated with the religious traditions of the Kukke Subrahmanya area.

Read more about Adi Subrahmanya

Devotees may wish to enquire locally about visiting this shrine as part of their pilgrimage. Check current access, timings and any applicable temple guidelines before finalising your itinerary.

Kukke Subrahmanya

Biladwara

A local religious landmark linked with traditions and legends associated with Kukke Subrahmanya.

Read more about Biladwara

Visitors interested in the stories surrounding Kukke may wish to include Biladwara if time and access allow. Ask locally about the best way to visit and whether any restrictions apply on your travel date.

Suggested Travel Plan

2-Day Dharmasthala & Kukke Itinerary

A sample plan that can be adjusted to suit your pickup point, darshan timings, travel date and group requirements.

Day 1

Mangalore to Dharmasthala

Start from your agreed pickup point and travel to Dharmasthala. Visit Sri Manjunatha Swamy Temple and, if time permits, explore the Bahubali statue at Ratnagiri Hill and Manjusha Museum. Continue to your planned overnight stay after completing the day's visits.

Day 2

Dharmasthala to Kukke Subrahmanya

Travel to Kukke Subrahmanya for temple darshan. If time, access and local conditions permit, consider Adi Subrahmanya Temple, Biladwara or the Kumaradhara River area. Begin the return journey to your agreed drop-off point after completing the planned visits.

Planning note: This is a suggested itinerary, not a fixed timetable. Temple queues, ritual bookings, traffic, weather and road conditions can affect the schedule. If you want to visit both main temples in one day, discuss feasibility and timing in advance.

Should temple darshan or seva be booked in advance?

What should we confirm before departure?
