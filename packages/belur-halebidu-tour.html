Belur & Halebidu Heritage Tour

Travel from Mangalore to two of Karnataka's most important Hoysala heritage destinations. Belur and Halebidu are known for their extraordinary temple architecture, detailed stone carvings and historical significance.

Belur

Visit the famous Chennakeshava Temple at Belur, renowned for its intricate sculptures, detailed stone carvings and beautiful Hoysala architecture.

Halebidu

Explore the historic Hoysaleswara Temple at Halebidu and admire its remarkable sculptures and architectural details.

Hoysala Architecture

Experience the distinctive Hoysala style of architecture, including detailed sculptures, ornate pillars and beautifully carved temple walls.
