Explore Belur, Halebidu and Nearby Attractions

Belur and Halebidu are known for their extraordinary Hoysala architecture, stone sculptures and cultural heritage. Their temples offer visitors a chance to discover the craftsmanship and history of medieval Karnataka.

You can explore the principal temples in Belur and Halebidu, then consider nearby attractions such as Belavadi or Yagachi Dam depending on your available time and travel plans.
