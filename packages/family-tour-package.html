Heritage

Mysore Palace

Discover royal architecture and the cultural heritage of Mysore, one of Karnataka's best-known holiday cities.

Explore this attraction

Mysore Palace is a popular stop for families interested in history, architecture and royal traditions. Allow enough time for the visit and check current opening hours, entry arrangements and photography rules before travelling.

Hill Station

Coorg

Enjoy green hills, coffee-growing landscapes, viewpoints and a relaxed hill-station atmosphere.

Explore this destination

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, offers scenic drives and nature-based outings. Depending on your route, you may consider viewpoints, gardens and local attractions. Some places involve steps or uneven paths, so plan according to your family's needs.

Nature

Chikkamagaluru

A scenic destination known for green hills, coffee plantations and Western Ghats landscapes.

Explore this destination

Chikkamagaluru can suit families who enjoy nature and mountain scenery. Popular outings in the surrounding region may involve winding roads, changing weather and walking. Confirm access conditions for individual viewpoints before departure.

Coastal Holiday

Udupi and Malpe Beach

Combine a visit to Udupi's famous temple town with coastal scenery and a family outing at Malpe Beach.

Explore this destination

Udupi offers a mix of religious, cultural and coastal experiences. Malpe Beach is a popular seaside destination. Follow lifeguard instructions, observe swimming restrictions and check boat services and weather conditions locally.

Coastal Landmark

Murudeshwara

Visit the famous Shiva statue and temple complex beside the Arabian Sea.

Explore this attraction

Murudeshwara is a memorable stop for families exploring coastal Karnataka. Visitors can admire the large Shiva statue, temple architecture and sea views. Allow time for parking, walking and the temple visit.

Beach Destination

Gokarna

Explore a coastal town known for its temples, beaches and distinctive landscape.

Explore this destination

Gokarna can be included in a longer coastal Karnataka trip. Some beaches require walking along uneven paths or steep trails, so choose stops carefully if travelling with young children or senior family members.

Wildlife and Nature

Dandeli

Discover forest landscapes, river scenery and nature-based activities in the Dandeli region.

Explore this destination

Dandeli is an option for families interested in nature and outdoor experiences. Activities such as rafting have age, weather and safety requirements. Confirm operating conditions and suitability before making plans.

Architecture and History

Belur and Halebidu

Discover celebrated Hoysala temple architecture and intricate stone carvings.

Explore these heritage sites

Belur and Halebidu are rewarding stops for families interested in Karnataka's history and craftsmanship. The temples feature detailed sculptures and architectural designs. Allow time for walking around the complexes and learning about the sites.

Pilgrimage

Dharmasthala

Visit Sri Manjunatha Swamy Temple and nearby cultural landmarks in this well-known pilgrimage destination.

Explore this destination

Dharmasthala is often included in family pilgrimage itineraries. You can discuss adding the Bahubali statue at Ratnagiri Hill or other local attractions. Temple queues and opening arrangements can affect the time required for your visit.

Pilgrimage

Kukke Subrahmanya

Visit the revered Kukke Subrahmanya Temple in the scenic Western Ghats region.

Explore this destination

Families travelling for religious purposes can plan temple darshan and enquire about specific sevas through official temple channels. Check current arrangements in advance, especially during festivals and busy periods.

Waterfalls

Jog Falls

Experience one of Karnataka's best-known waterfall destinations in the Western Ghats region.

Explore this attraction

Jog Falls is particularly impressive during suitable water-flow periods. The appearance of the falls changes with the season. Check viewpoint access, weather and local safety notices before visiting.

Wildlife

Mysore Zoo

Consider a family visit to one of Mysore's popular wildlife attractions.

Explore this attraction

Mysore Zoo can be a rewarding outing for families with children. Visiting involves considerable walking, so wear comfortable footwear and plan breaks. Check the zoo's current opening hours and weekly closure information before your visit.

Sample Holiday Plan

Suggested 5-Day Karnataka Family Tour

This is an example itinerary. The final route should be selected according to your pickup point and interests.

Day 1

Mangalore to Udupi and Coastal Karnataka

Start from your agreed pickup point. Depending on your interests, visit Udupi, Malpe Beach or other nearby coastal attractions. Overnight stay to be arranged according to your plan.

Day 2

Travel to Coorg

Enjoy a scenic journey towards Coorg. Select suitable viewpoints and local attractions depending on travel time, weather and your family's preferences.

Day 3

Coorg to Mysore

Spend time exploring selected Coorg attractions before travelling to Mysore, or dedicate more time to Mysore if you prefer a relaxed sightseeing schedule.

Day 4

Mysore Sightseeing

Visit Mysore Palace and consider Mysore Zoo or other local attractions according to opening hours and available time.

Day 5

Return Journey

Begin the return journey to your agreed drop-off point. A different destination or return route can be discussed before the trip is confirmed.

Important: This sample combines destinations across different regions, so driving time can be substantial. For a more relaxed family holiday, consider choosing either a coastal itinerary or a Coorg–Mysore itinerary instead of trying to cover too many places in a short trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Karnataka Family Tour FAQs

Which places are best for a family tour in Karnataka?

Popular options include Mysore, Coorg, Chikkamagaluru, Udupi, Murudeshwara, Gokarna, Belur, Halebidu and selected nature destinations. Choose places based on your family's interests and available days.

Can we customise the package?

Yes. You can discuss your preferred destinations, pickup point, travel dates, trip duration and vehicle requirements before confirming your itinerary.

Is this tour suitable for children and senior citizens?

It can be planned for families of different ages. Share any mobility requirements in advance and choose a manageable route with suitable rest breaks.

How many days should we plan?

A short regional holiday may take two or three days, while a multi-region Karnataka trip may need five days or longer. The ideal duration depends on the destinations you select and how much sightseeing you want.

What is included in the taxi package?

Confirm the quotation details before booking, including vehicle, kilometres or route, driver allowance, tolls, parking, accommodation requirements and any additional charges that may apply.

How can I get the family tour price?

Send your travel dates, pickup and drop-off points, passenger count, preferred destinations and number of days through WhatsApp or phone to request a quotation.
