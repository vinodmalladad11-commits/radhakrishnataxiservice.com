Explore Chikkamagaluru and Its Scenic Surroundings

Chikkamagaluru is known for its coffee-growing landscapes, green hills, mountain viewpoints and peaceful natural surroundings. It is a popular destination for travellers looking to enjoy Karnataka's Western Ghats.

Depending on your schedule and local road conditions, you can plan visits to Mullayanagiri, Baba Budangiri, Hirekolale Lake, Jhari Falls and other attractions in the region.
