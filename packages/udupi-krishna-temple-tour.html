Visit Sri Krishna Matha and Nearby Temples

Udupi Sri Krishna Matha is one of Karnataka's well-known pilgrimage destinations. The temple is associated with the 13th-century saint and philosopher Sri Madhvacharya and is renowned for devotees receiving darshan of Lord Krishna through the Navagraha Kindi.

The surrounding temple area includes the historic Chandramouleshwara and Anantheshwara temples. With more time, visitors can explore Pajaka Kshetra, associated with Sri Madhvacharya, or add coastal destinations such as Malpe Beach and Kapu Beach.

Radhakrishna Taxi Service can help you plan the journey from Mangalore, choose suitable stops and discuss a vehicle based on your group size. Your final route can be tailored to focus on temple darshan or include additional sightseeing.

Places to Visit on Your Udupi Tour

Click each place to read more about its significance and how it can fit into your itinerary.

1. Udupi Sri Krishna Matha

The main destination of the tour, Sri Krishna Matha is dedicated to Lord Krishna in the form of Balakrishna. Its worship traditions and long association with Sri Madhvacharya make it an important pilgrimage centre.

Allow sufficient time for darshan and follow the current instructions provided by temple staff.

Official Temple Website →
2. Navagraha Kindi

The Navagraha Kindi is the distinctive window through which devotees view Lord Krishna at Udupi Sri Krishna Matha. Its nine openings are a notable feature of the temple's darshan tradition.

Follow the designated queue and temple instructions. Photography restrictions apply in certain sacred areas; obey the current signs and staff directions.

Temple Visitor Information →
3. Kanakana Kindi

Kanakana Kindi is associated with the devotional story of the saint Kanakadasa and his longing for darshan of Lord Krishna. The place is an important part of Udupi's religious and cultural heritage.

Visitors can learn about the tradition while respecting the temple's customs and visitor flow.

4. Sri Chandramouleshwara Temple

This historic Shiva temple is situated close to Sri Krishna Matha. It is commonly included in a visit to the central Udupi temple area.

Devotees traditionally visit Chandramouleshwara before Krishna darshan. Check local instructions and temple timings for your visit.

5. Sri Anantheshwara Temple

Anantheshwara Temple is another important historic shrine near Sri Krishna Matha. Its association with Udupi's spiritual history makes it a worthwhile stop for pilgrims exploring the temple quarter.

This temple can usually be planned as part of the same central Udupi visit, depending on darshan time and local access arrangements.

6. Madhva Sarovara

Madhva Sarovara is the sacred water tank associated with the Krishna Matha complex. It is an important part of the temple's spiritual setting and history.

Follow posted rules around the water and temple premises. Do not enter restricted areas.

7. Ashta Mathas of Udupi

Udupi is closely associated with eight mathas connected with the worship traditions established by Sri Madhvacharya. Their history is central to the religious identity of the town.

If you are interested in the matha tradition, ask for additional time in the itinerary. Access to individual premises and activities may vary.

8. Pajaka Kshetra

Pajaka Kshetra, near Udupi, is traditionally associated with the birthplace of Sri Madhvacharya. It offers pilgrims an opportunity to explore places connected with his life and teachings.

This is an additional excursion, so allow for road travel and local visiting time when planning your day.

9. Malpe Beach

Malpe Beach is a popular coastal destination near Udupi. It can be added after temple darshan if your schedule permits and you would like a relaxed seaside stop.

Sea conditions and local safety instructions may affect beach activities. Boat services and island trips, where available, require separate timing and availability checks.

10. St. Mary's Island — Optional

St. Mary's Island is known for its distinctive geological formations off the Malpe coast. It is an optional sightseeing addition rather than a temple stop.

Access depends on authorised boat services, sea conditions and operating schedules. Confirm availability before including it in your trip plan.

11. Kapu Beach and Lighthouse

Kapu Beach offers a coastal setting south of Udupi, with a lighthouse that is a recognisable local landmark. It can be included on a longer temple-and-coast itinerary.

Lighthouse access may depend on opening hours, weather and local restrictions. Confirm access before visiting.

12. Anegudde Sri Vinayaka Temple

Anegudde Sri Vinayaka Temple is a recognised Ganesha pilgrimage destination in the wider Udupi district. It may be suitable for visitors planning a wider regional temple circuit.

Because it adds road travel, it is best considered when you have sufficient time beyond the main Krishna Matha visit.

13. Maravanthe Beach — Extended Trip

Maravanthe is known for its scenic coastal road and the distinctive landscape around the sea and river. It is farther away and is better suited to an extended coastal sightseeing itinerary.

Allow additional driving time rather than trying to fit every distant attraction into a short temple visit.

14. Kollur Sri Mookambika Temple — Extended Trip

Kollur Sri Mookambika Temple is a major Devi pilgrimage destination in the Western Ghats region. It can be included in a separate multi-day temple circuit that also covers selected coastal or Malnad destinations.

It requires additional road travel and may not be practical alongside all Udupi attractions in a single day.

Before you leave

Helpful Tips for Udupi Temple Visitors

Plan ahead for a smoother and more respectful pilgrimage.

Check Darshan Timings

Temple schedules can vary with rituals, festivals and special occasions. Check the official temple website close to your travel date rather than relying only on general online timings.

Check Official Information →
Follow Temple Dress and Conduct Rules

Wear modest clothing and follow the latest dress, footwear, photography and electronic-device rules specified by the temple administration.

Allow Time for Darshan

Crowd levels may increase during weekends, holidays and festivals. Keep your schedule flexible and avoid booking too many distant sightseeing stops on the same day.

Confirm Meals and Accommodation

Ask about available meal arrangements and accommodation if you plan to stay overnight. Do not assume these services are included in a taxi quotation.
