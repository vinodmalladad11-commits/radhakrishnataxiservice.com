Suggested Travel Plan

Coorg Tour Itinerary

Choose a short getaway or a longer trip. This sample three-day plan can be adjusted for your pickup point, interests and travel pace.

Day 1

Mangaluru to Madikeri

Start from Mangaluru and travel towards Coorg. After reaching Madikeri, explore Omkareshwara Temple, Madikeri Fort and Raja's Seat, subject to arrival time and opening hours. Enjoy the town and check in to your independently arranged accommodation.

Day 2

Waterfalls and Mountain Views

Visit Abbey Falls and plan a trip to Mandalpatti Viewpoint if weather and access conditions permit. You can also add a coffee estate visit or other nearby sightseeing stops. Allow extra time for local transport where required.

Day 3

Kushalnagar and Bylakuppe

Explore the Golden Temple at Namdroling Monastery, then consider Kaveri Nisargadhama or Dubare Elephant Camp, depending on available time and current visitor arrangements. Begin the return journey according to your drop location.

Optional Extension

Talacauvery and Bhagamandala

Add an extra day for Talacauvery and Bhagamandala. These destinations are in a different part of Kodagu, so a separate day may provide a more comfortable experience than combining them with a packed Madikeri sightseeing schedule.

Route planning: Coorg's attractions are spread across different parts of the district. Your final itinerary should account for driving time, road conditions, opening hours, weather and any local vehicle-access rules.
Travel Essentials

Tips for Visiting Coorg

Plan Your Visit

  • October to March is often popular for comfortable sightseeing weather.
  • Monsoon months bring lush greenery, but heavy rain can affect roads and waterfall access.
  • Carry light rain protection and comfortable footwear.
  • Check Mandalpatti access and local jeep requirements before travelling.
  • Reserve wildlife safaris and special visitor activities in advance where applicable.
  • Allow time for meals, breaks and the winding hill roads.
