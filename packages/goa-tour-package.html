North Goa • Beach

Calangute Beach

One of Goa's well-known beaches, popular for its lively atmosphere, coastal views and nearby shops.

Read more about Calangute Beach

Calangute is a busy tourist area with restaurants, shops and beachside activities. It can be crowded during holidays and peak season. Follow local swimming advice, observe beach safety flags and check activity prices before participating.

North Goa • Beach

Baga Beach

A popular coastal destination with restaurants, seaside walks and a lively holiday atmosphere.

Read more about Baga Beach

Baga is known for its busy beachfront and surrounding entertainment areas. Traffic and parking can be challenging during busy periods, so allow extra time. Water activities depend on weather, local permissions and operator safety arrangements.

North Goa • Fort

Fort Aguada

A historic Portuguese-era fort overlooking the Arabian Sea.

Read more about Fort Aguada

Fort Aguada is a notable heritage attraction associated with Goa's colonial history and coastal defences. Visitors can explore selected accessible areas and enjoy sea views. Check current visitor access and allow time for walking around the site.

North Goa • Scenic

Chapora Fort

A hilltop fort known for coastal views and its elevated location above the surrounding landscape.

Read more about Chapora Fort

Chapora Fort offers panoramic views of the surrounding coastline. The approach involves an uphill walk over uneven ground, so wear suitable footwear and take care in wet weather. It may not be suitable for everyone with limited mobility.

North Goa • Beach

Anjuna Beach

A coastal destination known for its rocky scenery, beach atmosphere and nearby markets.

Read more about Anjuna Beach

Anjuna has a distinctive coastal landscape and a long association with Goa's tourism culture. Explore the area at a relaxed pace and check local market days and opening times if shopping is part of your itinerary.

North Goa • Beach

Vagator Beach

A scenic beach area known for rocky headlands and views of the Arabian Sea.

Read more about Vagator Beach

Vagator offers attractive coastal scenery and opportunities for photography. Some access routes involve steps or uneven terrain. Check sea conditions and local safety instructions, and avoid unsafe cliff edges.

Heritage • Old Goa

Basilica of Bom Jesus

A renowned historic church and an important heritage landmark in Old Goa.

Read more about the basilica

The Basilica of Bom Jesus is recognised for its historical and architectural significance. Visitors should dress respectfully, observe church rules and check current visiting arrangements. Combine the visit with nearby heritage sites when time allows.

Heritage • Old Goa

Se Cathedral

A significant church in Old Goa known for its grand architecture and historic setting.

Read more about Se Cathedral

Se Cathedral is one of Old Goa's notable religious and heritage buildings. Its scale and architecture make it a worthwhile stop for travellers interested in history. Respect worshippers and follow posted visitor guidelines.

Capital City • Heritage

Fontainhas, Panaji

Explore the Latin Quarter of Panaji, known for colourful heritage houses and narrow lanes.

Read more about Fontainhas

Fontainhas is a distinctive neighbourhood with Portuguese-influenced architecture and colourful buildings. It is best explored slowly and on foot. Be considerate of residents because many buildings are private homes.

South Goa • Beach

Colva Beach

A popular South Goa beach suitable for a coastal walk and a relaxed seaside visit.

Read more about Colva Beach

Colva is a well-known beach destination with nearby food and accommodation options. Conditions can vary with the season, and swimming may be restricted at times. Follow lifeguard advice and check local notices before entering the water.

South Goa • Beach

Palolem Beach

A scenic crescent-shaped beach in South Goa with a relaxed coastal atmosphere.

Read more about Palolem Beach

Palolem is often chosen by travellers looking for a quieter beach experience. Reaching South Goa attractions from North Goa can require substantial driving time. Consider staying overnight nearby if Palolem is a priority.

South Goa • Nature

Dudhsagar Falls

A famous waterfall in the Western Ghats region near Goa's eastern border.

Read more before planning a visit

Dudhsagar Falls access depends on season, official restrictions and authorised transport arrangements. A regular taxi may not be permitted to take visitors all the way to the waterfall. Confirm current access, permits, authorised transfers and weather conditions before including it in your plan.

Shopping

Mapusa Market

A traditional market area offering a glimpse of local shopping and everyday Goan life.

Read more about Mapusa Market

Mapusa Market is a useful stop for visitors interested in local produce, household items, regional products and shopping. Market activity varies by day and time, so plan your visit around your chosen sightseeing route.

North Goa • Fort

Reis Magos Fort

A restored historic fort overlooking the Mandovi River and the surrounding coastline.

Read more about Reis Magos Fort

Reis Magos Fort offers a heritage experience with river views and restored historic spaces. Check its current opening hours and entry arrangements before adding it to your route.

South Goa • Heritage

South Goa Heritage Churches

Discover historic religious buildings and the cultural heritage of Goa's southern region.

Read more about heritage visits

South Goa has churches and heritage locations that can be included depending on your interests and route. Confirm visitor access and service timings locally. Please dress respectfully and observe restrictions during worship.

Sample Holiday Plan

Suggested 3-Day Goa Tour Itinerary

A sample schedule that can be adapted to your pickup point, overnight stays and preferred attractions.

Day 1

Mangalore to Goa

Start from your agreed pickup location and travel to Goa. After check-in or arrival arrangements, explore a suitable nearby beach or local attraction depending on arrival time.

Day 2

North Goa Sightseeing

Select attractions such as Fort Aguada, Calangute Beach, Baga Beach, Chapora Fort, Anjuna or Vagator. The exact combination depends on traffic, access and how much time you want at each place.

Day 3

Heritage or South Goa and Return

Depending on your return time, visit selected heritage sites in Old Goa or a suitable nearby attraction. South Goa beaches generally need additional travel time, so plan an overnight stay or a longer itinerary if they are a priority.

Planning note: Goa's North and South attractions are spread out. For a relaxed holiday, consider spending one day in North Goa and a separate day in South Goa rather than attempting every attraction in a single trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Goa Tour Package FAQs

How many days are enough for a Goa tour from Mangalore?

Two or three days can cover selected attractions, depending on your interests and travel schedule. A longer trip is better if you want to explore both North Goa and South Goa at a relaxed pace.

Can I customise my Goa tour package?

Yes. You can choose preferred beaches, forts, churches, heritage attractions and markets. Discuss the route and number of days before confirming the trip.

Can we visit North Goa and South Goa together?

Yes, but travel time between the regions can be substantial. A longer itinerary with an overnight stay is often more comfortable than rushing between distant attractions.

Is Dudhsagar Falls included automatically?

No. Access depends on current rules, authorised transport, permits and seasonal conditions. Confirm arrangements before including the waterfall in your plan.

How much does the Goa tour cost?

The quotation depends on pickup and drop-off points, trip duration, vehicle type, kilometres and agreed itinerary. Contact us with your requirements for a personalised fare estimate.

What details should I send to get a quotation?
