About this tour

One Journey, Many Sides of Karnataka

A flexible road-trip plan for families, couples, groups and travellers who want to explore more than one destination.

Karnataka brings together historic cities, traditional architecture, coffee-growing hills, spiritual destinations, forests and a scenic Arabian Sea coastline. This multi-day tour is designed to help you combine selected destinations into one planned journey, with time for sightseeing and rest.

The sample route starts from Mangalore and includes Mysuru, Coorg, Chikkamagaluru, Belur, Halebidu and selected coastal destinations. You can adjust the route according to your available days, interests and travel budget.

Travel planning tip: Karnataka is a large state. Covering every destination in one trip can involve long drives. This seven-day plan is a suggested route, not a fixed schedule; confirm driving times, overnight stops and the final route before booking.
Explore the destinations

Places You Can Include in Your Tour

Select any destination below to read more about its attractions. Your final itinerary can include the places that interest you most.

1. Mysuru – Royal Heritage and Culture

Mysuru is known for its royal history, grand architecture, cultural traditions and lively markets. It is a good choice for travellers interested in heritage and family sightseeing.

Places to consider:

Mysuru Palace Chamundi Hill Mysuru Zoo St. Philomena's Church Brindavan Gardens

Allow sufficient time for palace visits and traffic. Check current opening hours and garden timings before travel.

2. Coorg – Coffee Estates and Green Hills

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, offers forested hills, coffee plantations, viewpoints and waterfalls. It is suitable for a relaxed nature-focused holiday.

Places to consider:

Madikeri Fort Raja's Seat Abbey Falls Talacauvery Namdroling Monastery

Some attractions are far apart. Choose a practical combination instead of trying to visit every place in one day.

3. Chikkamagaluru – Coffee Country

Chikkamagaluru is known for coffee-growing landscapes, mountain views and scenic drives through the Western Ghats. The region suits nature lovers and travellers looking for a break from busy cities.

Places to consider:

Mullayanagiri Baba Budangiri Manikyadhara Falls Hirekolale Lake Coffee estates

Hill roads and viewpoints may be affected by weather, local access rules or traffic. Check access conditions before setting out.

4. Belur – Hoysala Temple Architecture

Belur is an important heritage destination known for detailed stone carvings and Hoysala temple architecture. Visitors can appreciate the craftsmanship, sculptures and historic setting.

Places to consider:

Chennakeshava Temple Temple sculptures Historic town centre

Dress respectfully at religious sites and follow temple photography and visitor rules.

5. Halebidu – Historic Hoysala Heritage

Halebidu is celebrated for its remarkable temple architecture and intricate sculptural details. It pairs naturally with Belur for a heritage sightseeing day.

Places to consider:

Hoysaleswara Temple Kedareshwara Temple Archaeological surroundings

Belur and Halebidu can be planned together, subject to your route and available sightseeing time.

6. Udupi – Temples and Coastal Culture

Udupi combines religious heritage, local cuisine and coastal scenery. It is a useful stop on a journey along Karnataka's western coastline.

Places to consider:

Sri Krishna Matha Malpe Beach Kaup Lighthouse St. Mary's Islands

Boat trips to St. Mary's Islands depend on operating schedules, sea conditions and official access arrangements. Confirm availability before planning your visit.

7. Murudeshwara – Temple and Sea Views

Murudeshwara is known for its prominent Shiva statue, temple complex and coastal setting. It is a popular stop for pilgrims, families and coastal road travellers.

Places to consider:

Murudeshwara Temple Shiva statue Coastal viewpoints

Allow extra time during weekends, holidays and festival periods when visitor traffic may be heavier.

8. Gokarna – Beaches and Sacred Heritage

Gokarna offers a mix of religious sites, relaxed beach scenery and coastal walks. It can be included for travellers who want a slower-paced coastal experience.

Places to consider:

Mahabaleshwar Temple Maha Ganapati Temple Kudle Beach Om Beach Mirjan Fort

Beach walks and boat activities depend on local conditions. Follow safety instructions and respect temple customs.

9. Dharmasthala – A Spiritual Destination

Dharmasthala is a major pilgrimage destination in Dakshina Kannada, known for Sri Manjunatha Swamy Temple and its tradition of hospitality.

Places to consider:

Sri Manjunatha Temple Bahubali Statue Manjusha Museum

Temple queues and visitor procedures can vary. Keep your schedule flexible and check current guidance.

10. Kukke Subrahmanya – Temple and Western Ghats

Kukke Subrahmanya is a revered temple destination surrounded by the Western Ghats. It can be added to a spiritual tour with Dharmasthala or nearby destinations.

Places to consider:

Kukke Subrahmanya Temple Kumaradhara River area Western Ghats scenery

Check temple timings and ritual arrangements directly with the temple authorities if your visit includes specific ceremonies.

11. Hampi – Vijayanagara Heritage

Hampi is renowned for its historic ruins, temples, boulder landscapes and Vijayanagara-era monuments. It is an excellent option for a dedicated heritage tour.

Places to consider:

Virupaksha Temple Vittala Temple complex Stone Chariot Hampi Bazaar

Hampi is far from the coastal and southern destinations in this sample route. It is usually better to add extra days or plan a separate northern Karnataka itinerary.

12. Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal – Heritage Circuit

Northern Karnataka offers a distinctive heritage circuit featuring rock-cut cave temples, historic temple complexes and early Indian architectural traditions.

Places to consider:

Badami Cave Temples Aihole temples Pattadakal monuments Agastya Lake

These destinations are best planned as a separate multi-day circuit, with suitable overnight stops.

Attraction access, opening hours, entry charges and local restrictions can change. Confirm the latest details before departure.

Mysuru Sightseeing and Transfer to Coorg

Mysuru Palace Optional city attractions Coorg
Day 3

Explore Coorg

Enjoy a day among coffee-growing landscapes and hill scenery. Select a practical combination of Madikeri attractions, viewpoints, waterfalls or a monastery. Stay overnight in Coorg.

Madikeri Raja's Seat Selected local attractions
Day 4

Coorg to Chikkamagaluru

Travel towards Chikkamagaluru. Enjoy the scenic drive and keep the evening relaxed. Depending on your arrival time, include a nearby lake or viewpoint.

Coorg Scenic road journey Chikkamagaluru
Day 5

Chikkamagaluru, Belur and Halebidu

Visit selected hill or coffee-country attractions in the morning if conditions permit. Continue towards Belur and Halebidu for Hoysala heritage sightseeing, then stay at a suitable location based on the next day's route.

Chikkamagaluru Belur Halebidu
Day 6

Travel to Udupi and Coastal Sightseeing

Head towards the coast. Depending on the travel time, visit Udupi Krishna Matha, Malpe Beach or another nearby attraction. Stay overnight around Udupi or along the coast.

Udupi Krishna Matha Malpe Beach
Day 7

Murudeshwara and Gokarna – Route Planning Required

If you wish to include Murudeshwara and Gokarna, discuss the timing and final drop location before confirming the package. Visiting both places and returning to Mangalore on the same day may make the journey long. An additional day or a different drop point may be more comfortable.

Murudeshwara Gokarna (optional) Confirm final drop

Frequently asked questions

Karnataka Tour Package FAQs

How many days are recommended for a Karnataka multi-day tour?

Seven days is one possible starting plan for selected destinations. The ideal duration depends on your pickup point, chosen destinations, sightseeing preferences and travel pace. Larger circuits such as Hampi and Badami need additional planning.

Can I customise the Karnataka tour itinerary?

Yes. Discuss the number of days, destinations, pickup and drop locations, vehicle category and sightseeing preferences with Radhakrishna Taxi Service.

Is this package suitable for families?

The route can be adapted for families, couples and groups. Share the group size, children's ages if applicable, luggage needs and preferred travel pace before confirming the vehicle.

Are hotels, meals and entry tickets included?

These should be confirmed in your written quotation. Do not assume that hotels, meals, monument tickets, tolls, parking or other expenses are included in the taxi fare.

Can the tour start from Mangalore?

Mangalore is the suggested starting point for this sample itinerary. Ask about your preferred pickup point and the most practical route for your travel dates.

Can I include Hampi or Badami in the same trip?

Yes, but those destinations are in northern Karnataka and require extra driving time. For a comfortable experience, consider adding days or arranging a separate heritage circuit.

How can I get a quotation?

Contact Radhakrishna Taxi Service by WhatsApp, phone or email. Provide your travel dates, number of passengers, preferred destinations, pickup and drop locations and vehicle preference to help prepare a suitable quotation.
