About this tour

A Spiritual Journey Through Coastal Karnataka

Combine a visit to the sacred Kollur Mookambika Temple with Murudeshwara's coastal temple complex and selected nearby sightseeing places.

Kollur is home to Sri Mookambika Temple, an important pilgrimage destination set amid the greenery of the Western Ghats. Murudeshwara is known for its Shiva statue, temple complex and views of the Arabian Sea. Together, these places make a memorable short trip for families, devotees and travellers exploring coastal Karnataka.

This package provides a suggested two-day itinerary. The exact route, sightseeing stops, vehicle and travel schedule can be discussed according to your requirements.

Planning tip: If your main priority is temple darshan, allow enough time for queues and temple rituals. Kodachadri and other longer excursions are best planned as extensions rather than rushed into the same two-day schedule.

1. Sri Mookambika Temple, Kollur

Sri Mookambika Temple is the main pilgrimage destination of Kollur. Dedicated to Goddess Mookambika, the temple is nestled near the Western Ghats and attracts devotees throughout the year.

What to experience:

Temple darshan Traditional worship Spiritual atmosphere

Plan your visit around the temple's official worship schedule. The temple generally has a midday closure, and special days may have different arrangements.

Official information: Kollur temple details and timings

2. Souparnika River Area

The Souparnika River is associated with the Kollur pilgrimage area. Visitors may enjoy the natural surroundings and the peaceful atmosphere near the river.

Suitable for:

Nature scenery Quiet moments Pilgrimage visit

River conditions vary with rainfall and season. Follow local guidance and avoid entering the water in unsafe conditions.

3. Kodachadri Hills – Optional Extension

Kodachadri is a scenic mountain destination in the Western Ghats near Kollur. It is known for forest landscapes, hill views and outdoor excursions.

Highlights:

Western Ghats Mountain scenery Nature excursion

Kodachadri generally needs substantial extra time. Local jeep access, route conditions, weather and permissions must be checked before planning. It is not automatically included in the standard two-day package.

4. Murudeshwara Temple

Murudeshwara Temple stands on Kanduka Hill beside the Arabian Sea. The temple complex is an important religious stop on Karnataka's coastal route.

Highlights:

Temple darshan Kanduka Hill Sea views

Allow time for the temple visit and follow the current dress code and visitor instructions provided by the temple authorities.

5. Murudeshwara Shiva Statue

The prominent Shiva statue is one of Murudeshwara's best-known landmarks. Set beside the sea, it forms a striking part of the temple complex and attracts visitors for its scale and surroundings.

Visitor experiences:

Statue viewing Photography outdoors Coastal scenery

Photography restrictions may apply inside specific religious areas. Follow posted rules and instructions from temple staff.

6. Murudeshwara Raja Gopuram

The tall Raja Gopuram is a prominent feature of the Murudeshwara temple entrance. Visitors can appreciate its architecture and the surrounding coastal views.

If the viewing lift is operating and you wish to use it, check its current availability, charges and any visitor restrictions on arrival.

Temple architecture Panoramic views
7. Murudeshwara Beach

Murudeshwara Beach lies beside the temple complex and offers a coastal setting for a walk and sea views.

Good for:

Beach walk Coastal photographs Relaxation

Sea conditions can be dangerous even when the shore appears calm. Follow local safety warnings and do not enter restricted areas.

8. Maravanthe Beach and Coastal Highway

Maravanthe is known for its scenic coastal road setting, with the Arabian Sea on one side and the Souparnika River on the other. It makes an attractive stop when the route and schedule allow.

Highlights:

Scenic drive Sea views Photo stop

Stop only at safe, permitted parking areas. Do not stand on the road or obstruct traffic to take photographs.

9. Byndoor and Someshwara Beach Area

The Byndoor coastal area offers additional sea views and a quieter coastal atmosphere. Selected nearby beach stops may be included if time and road conditions permit.

Coastal scenery Relaxed stop

Confirm the exact beach location with your driver and check local access and sea safety conditions before visiting.

10. Apsarakonda – Optional Coastal Excursion

Apsarakonda, in the Honnavar region, is known for its waterfall and coastal surroundings. It may be considered as an optional extension if you have additional time.

Waterfall scenery Nature outing

Access, water levels and safety conditions change with the season. Avoid slippery areas and follow local instructions.

11. Jog Falls – Longer Optional Extension

Jog Falls is a major waterfall destination in Karnataka's Western Ghats region. It can be added to a longer trip, but requires additional road travel and sightseeing time.

Waterfall viewpoint Nature scenery

It is not part of the standard two-day Kollur–Murudeshwara itinerary. Discuss an extra day and the route before booking. Waterfall views vary with season and rainfall.

Attraction access, timings, local transport arrangements, entry charges and weather conditions may change. Confirm the latest details before travel.

Suggested travel plan

2-Day Kollur Murudeshwara Itinerary

A practical starting plan from Mangalore, with time for temple visits and selected coastal sightseeing.

Day 1

Mangalore to Kollur – Mookambika Temple Darshan

Begin from your confirmed pickup location in Mangalore and travel towards Kollur. Visit Sri Mookambika Temple, allowing enough time for darshan and temple procedures. If time permits, explore the nearby river area and local surroundings. Stay overnight in Kollur or a nearby suitable town.

Mangalore pickup Kollur Mookambika Temple Overnight stay
Day 2

Kollur to Murudeshwara – Temple and Coastal Views

After breakfast, travel towards Murudeshwara. Visit the temple complex, Shiva statue and Raja Gopuram viewing area if available. Depending on your onward route and remaining time, consider a stop at Maravanthe Beach before continuing to your agreed drop location.

Frequently asked questions

Kollur Murudeshwara Tour FAQs

How many days are needed for Kollur and Murudeshwara?

Two days and one night is a suggested plan for Kollur and Murudeshwara. Extra time may be needed for Kodachadri, additional sightseeing or a more relaxed schedule.

Can the tour start from Mangalore?

Yes. Mangalore is the suggested starting point for this package. Confirm your exact pickup location and preferred final drop when requesting a quotation.

Can Kodachadri be included?

Kodachadri can be discussed as an optional extension. It needs additional time, and local transport arrangements, weather and access conditions should be checked before travel.

Are hotel rooms included in the package?

Hotel accommodation is not automatically included. Ask for a quotation that clearly states whether accommodation and meals are included or excluded.

Are temple pooja and seva charges included?

Pooja, seva, offerings and other temple-related expenses should be checked separately. Confirm the arrangements with the temple authorities and your tour provider.

Can we visit Maravanthe Beach during this tour?

Maravanthe can be considered as a coastal stop depending on the route, daylight and final drop location. Confirm the sightseeing plan when booking your vehicle.
