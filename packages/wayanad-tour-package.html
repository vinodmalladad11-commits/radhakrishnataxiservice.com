Popular Places to Visit in Wayanad

Click any attraction to reveal more information. Select the places that interest you when planning your Wayanad taxi tour.

1. Edakkal Caves

Edakkal Caves are famous for prehistoric engravings and rock art that offer a glimpse into ancient human life. The approach involves an uphill walk, steps and uneven terrain, so comfortable footwear and a reasonable level of fitness are recommended.

Entry is subject to the site's opening schedule, ticket availability and access rules. Check the latest timings before setting out.

History and trekking
2. Banasura Sagar Dam

Banasura Sagar Dam is known for its reservoir, surrounding green hills and scenic landscape. It is a popular stop for photography and enjoying Wayanad's natural surroundings.

Boating and other activities, where offered, depend on weather, operating hours and local safety instructions.

Scenic views
3. Pookode Lake

Pookode Lake is a freshwater lake surrounded by forested hills near Vythiri. Visitors often enjoy the peaceful atmosphere, lakeside scenery and nature photography.

Boating and other visitor facilities may be available according to the current operating schedule.

Lake and nature
4. Soochipara Waterfalls

Soochipara, also known as Sentinel Rock Waterfalls, is a multi-tiered waterfall set amid forested surroundings. The walk to the falls may involve slopes and steps.

Access can be restricted during heavy rain or unsafe conditions. Follow local instructions and do not enter prohibited swimming areas.

Waterfall
5. Chembra Peak

Chembra Peak is one of Wayanad's best-known mountain landmarks, attracting visitors for its elevated landscape and trekking experience. The trek is not simply a drive-up viewpoint; access involves an organised walking route.

Trekking permission, route access, visitor limits and weather restrictions may apply. Confirm current rules with the responsible local authorities before planning this stop.

Trekking · Permission may be required
6. Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary – Muthanga

Muthanga is a forest area associated with Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary and its rich biodiversity. The wider region is home to a variety of wildlife, including elephants and numerous bird species.

Safari access, if operating, follows official schedules and forest department rules. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, and visitors must not enter restricted forest areas independently.

Wildlife and forest
7. Tholpetty Wildlife Area

Tholpetty is another well-known wildlife viewing area associated with Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary. Its forest habitat makes it an option for nature enthusiasts who want to experience the region's biodiversity.

Safari timings, entry arrangements and availability must be checked in advance. Muthanga and Tholpetty are in different parts of the region, so visiting both may require extra travel time.

Wildlife experience
8. Kuruva Island

Kuruva Island, also called Kuruvadweep, is a river-island landscape known for greenery and natural surroundings. It is suited to visitors interested in a quiet nature experience.

Entry can be seasonal and subject to forest, environmental, weather and visitor-capacity restrictions. Confirm that the island is open before adding it to your itinerary.

Nature · Seasonal access
9. Lakkidi View Point

Lakkidi View Point overlooks the winding mountain roads and green valleys of the Western Ghats. It is a convenient scenic stop when travelling through the Vythiri–Lakkidi area.

Views are often especially atmospheric in misty weather, although visibility varies. Stop only in safe, permitted parking areas.

Viewpoint and photography
10. Phantom Rock

Phantom Rock is a distinctive natural rock formation near Ambalavayal, named for its resemblance to a skull. It is an interesting short stop for landscape photography and geology enthusiasts.

Access and the amount of walking required depend on the approach and current local conditions.

Natural rock formation
11. Kanthanpara Waterfalls

Kanthanpara Waterfalls is a picturesque waterfall surrounded by greenery. It can be a pleasant addition to a sightseeing route for visitors who enjoy natural landscapes without planning a full-day trek.

Water levels and safe access vary with the season. Follow local directions and avoid slippery rocks.

Waterfall and nature
12. Meenmutty Waterfalls

Meenmutty Waterfalls is known for its dramatic cascade and forested surroundings. The name is used for waterfalls in more than one location in Kerala, so confirm the exact Wayanad access point when planning your trip.

Some approaches require a walk over uneven terrain. Access may be limited during monsoon conditions or when authorities consider the route unsafe.

Waterfall · Check access
13. Karapuzha Dam

Karapuzha Dam is a scenic reservoir area surrounded by the rolling landscapes of Wayanad. It can be included in a relaxed sightseeing itinerary focused on countryside views and photography.

Visitor access and recreational facilities depend on the areas open to the public at the time of your visit.

Dam and countryside
14. Thirunelli Temple

Thirunelli Temple is an ancient temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, set in a forested mountain landscape in northern Wayanad. It is a significant spiritual destination and an option for travellers interested in heritage and pilgrimage.

This temple is relatively remote from several southern Wayanad attractions. Allow sufficient driving time and check temple timings before visiting.

Temple and heritage
15. Jain Temple, Sultan Bathery

The historic Jain Temple at Sultan Bathery is associated with Wayanad's Jain heritage and traditional architecture. The site offers a cultural stop for visitors exploring the Sultan Bathery area.

Respect the site's heritage status and any access or photography restrictions that may be in place.

History and architecture
16. Wayanad Heritage Museum

Wayanad Heritage Museum at Ambalavayal showcases aspects of the region's archaeology, traditional culture and historical life through collections of artefacts and heritage displays.

It is a useful stop for travellers who want to understand the cultural history of Wayanad alongside its natural attractions. Confirm current opening hours before arrival.

Museum and local history
17. En Ooru Tribal Heritage Village

En Ooru, near Pookode, is a heritage and cultural destination intended to introduce visitors to aspects of indigenous communities and traditional architecture.

Visitor activities and entry arrangements can change. Visit respectfully, follow site rules and avoid treating community life as a staged attraction.

Culture and heritage
18. Chain Tree, Lakkidi

Chain Tree is a well-known landmark near Lakkidi associated with local legends about the mountain road. It is a brief cultural stop often combined with nearby viewpoints.

Park safely and do not stop in traffic lanes on the winding ghat road.

Local legend and landmark
19. Neelimala View Point

Neelimala is known for expansive views of green valleys and the surrounding hill country. It is an option for travellers who want to add a scenic viewpoint to their Wayanad itinerary.

Access may involve walking or local transport arrangements. Check the current route, permissions and weather before including this stop.

Scenic landscape
20. Makkiyad Meenmutty Waterfalls

Makkiyad Meenmutty Waterfalls is a waterfall attraction in the northern part of Wayanad, offering a different setting from some of the better-known southern routes. It may appeal to visitors seeking a quieter nature stop.

Verify the exact location, local access conditions and current opening status when planning. Do not confuse it with other waterfalls sharing the Meenmutty name.

Northern Wayanad nature stop
21. Sultan Bathery Town

Sultan Bathery is a useful base for exploring several historic and natural attractions in southern Wayanad. Travellers can plan meal breaks, local shopping and visits to nearby heritage sites from here.

The town is also a practical stop for organising a route towards Edakkal Caves, the Jain Temple and nearby countryside attractions.

Town and local culture
22. Vythiri Hill Station Area

Vythiri is surrounded by lush hills, plantations and forested scenery. It is a popular accommodation area for visitors exploring Pookode Lake, Lakkidi and nearby nature attractions.

Travellers can use Vythiri as a base for a relaxed itinerary, depending on their selected stops and available time.

Hill scenery and stays
23. Pazhassi Raja Tomb

Pazhassi Raja Tomb, in the Mananthavady area, commemorates Kerala's historical ruler Pazhassi Raja, remembered for his resistance against British colonial rule.

It is a heritage stop for travellers interested in the history of northern Kerala. Confirm access and timings before visiting.

History and heritage
24. Valliyoorkavu Bhagavathy Temple

Valliyoorkavu Bhagavathy Temple near Mananthavady is a well-known regional temple with cultural significance. Visitors interested in local religious traditions can consider adding it to a northern Wayanad route.

Please respect temple customs and check the current schedule before your visit.

Temple and culture
Important: The attractions above are options, not a promise that every site can be covered in one trip. Some are far apart. Waterfalls, trekking routes, wildlife safaris and islands may be closed or restricted. We recommend choosing attractions by region to avoid unnecessary driving.

Before you travel

Useful Wayanad Travel Tips

Choose the right duration

Two or three days is a useful starting point for sightseeing. Add time if you want to cover distant northern attractions, trekking or wildlife activities.

Check weather and access

Heavy rain can affect waterfalls, mountain roads, treks and forest access. Confirm operating status before leaving.

Wear suitable footwear

Caves, viewpoints and waterfalls can involve steps, slopes or uneven ground. Carry comfortable walking shoes and rain protection when appropriate.

Book restricted activities early

Trekking, safari and island access may have permits, visitor limits or fixed time slots. Follow official booking and entry instructions.

Keep the route practical

Group attractions by area rather than crossing Wayanad repeatedly. Share your hotel location when requesting a taxi quotation.

Confirm your quotation

Discuss your pickup and drop points, dates, vehicle category, sightseeing plan, waiting time and any applicable extra charges before confirming the booking.
