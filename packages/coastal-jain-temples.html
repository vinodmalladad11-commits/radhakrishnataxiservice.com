Explore the Historic Jain Basadis of Coastal Karnataka

Coastal Karnataka is home to important Jain heritage sites, including historic basadis, traditional religious centres, finely carved stone structures and the celebrated Gomateshwara statue at Karkala.

Moodabidri is particularly known for its Jain temples and the Saavira Kambada Basadi, while Karkala is associated with Chaturmukha Basadi and its monumental Gomateshwara statue. Varanga offers another distinctive Jain heritage destination in the wider Udupi region.
