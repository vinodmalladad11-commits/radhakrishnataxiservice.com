Western Ghats Destinations and Attractions

Click each place to read about the destination and understand how it may fit into your itinerary.

Agumbe, Kudremukh, Kalasa, Sringeri, Charmadi Ghat and nearby rainforest scenery.

Chikkamagaluru Highlands

Mullayanagiri, Baba Budangiri, Kemmanagundi, coffee estates and mountain viewpoints.

Malnad and Forest Landscapes

Jog Falls, Bisle Ghat, Sakleshpur, Coorg and selected destinations towards Wayanad.

1. Kudremukh National Park

Kudremukh is renowned for its rolling grasslands, forested mountains, misty valleys and rich biodiversity. It is one of Karnataka's most distinctive Western Ghats landscapes.

The region is suitable for nature-focused travel. Trekking and certain forest activities require official permissions and must follow current regulations. A normal taxi sightseeing visit does not automatically include entry to restricted areas.

Mountains and biodiversity
2. Agumbe Rainforest Region

Agumbe is known for heavy rainfall, lush rainforest, winding ghat roads and dramatic hill scenery. Its surrounding landscape supports a rich variety of plants, amphibians, reptiles and birds.

It is a memorable drive for visitors interested in rainforest scenery. Take extra care during rain and follow local advice on road and trail conditions.

Rainforest and scenic drive
3. Agumbe Sunset Point

This popular viewpoint offers broad views over the Western Ghats and the coastal-facing landscape. Clear evenings can provide especially beautiful sunset views.

Plan arrival before sunset and park only in safe, permitted places. Cloud cover and monsoon weather can obscure the view.

Sunset viewpoint
4. Barkana Falls Viewpoint

Barkana Falls is a dramatic waterfall associated with the Seetha River and the forested Agumbe region. The surrounding valley offers an impressive glimpse of the Western Ghats' steep terrain.

Some approaches require walking on forest trails. Access may be unsafe during heavy rain, and local restrictions should always be followed.

Waterfall and forest valley
5. Jogi Gundi Waterfalls

Jogi Gundi is a waterfall attraction in the Agumbe region, surrounded by green forest scenery. It can be considered for visitors who want to explore the area's natural features.

Conditions on the approach can vary by season. Check local access information before travelling and avoid slippery rocks or restricted areas.

Waterfall
6. Kundadri Hills

Kundadri Hills is a scenic hill destination near Agumbe, known for sweeping views and a historic Jain temple dedicated to Parshwanath. The summit area offers a mix of heritage and natural beauty.

The access road is steep and narrow in places. Use caution on the climb and check weather conditions before visiting.

Hilltop and Jain heritage
7. Sringeri

Sringeri is a well-known pilgrimage town beside the Tunga River. Sri Sharadamba Temple and Vidyashankara Temple are important heritage and spiritual attractions.

Sringeri can be combined with selected nearby Malnad destinations, depending on the route and time available.

Temple and riverside town
8. Kalasa and Bhadra River

Kalasa is a scenic Malnad town surrounded by hills, plantations and river landscapes. Kalaseshwara Temple is a major local spiritual landmark, while the surrounding countryside offers attractive mountain-road views.

Kalasa can be planned alongside Horanadu, Kudremukh-region viewpoints and other nearby places, subject to road access.

Malnad and pilgrimage
9. Charmadi Ghat

Charmadi Ghat is a scenic mountain pass linking the coastal side of Karnataka with the Malnad region. The drive is known for green slopes, forest scenery, winding roads and seasonal mist.

Heavy rain, fog, fallen trees or landslides can affect travel. Allow extra time and check the latest road conditions before choosing this route.

Scenic mountain drive
10. Bisle Ghat Viewpoint

Bisle Ghat is known for panoramic views of the surrounding mountain ranges and forested valleys. The viewpoint is a rewarding stop for landscape photography and quiet nature appreciation.

Roads can be narrow and weather-dependent. Check access conditions and avoid entering restricted forest areas.

Panoramic mountain views
11. Jog Falls

Jog Falls is one of Karnataka's best-known waterfalls, where the Sharavathi River drops dramatically through a deep green landscape. The viewpoint area offers a memorable stop for visitors and photographers.

Water volume and appearance vary with reservoir releases and rainfall. Viewpoint access and walking routes should be checked before the visit.

Major waterfall
12. Mullayanagiri Peak

Mullayanagiri is the highest peak in Karnataka and a celebrated destination for mountain views, cool weather and short walks. Its elevated setting offers expansive views of the Chikkamagaluru hill ranges.

Conditions can be windy, misty or slippery. Check road access and any local visitor restrictions, and avoid unsafe edges.

Mountain summit
13. Baba Budangiri

Baba Budangiri is a mountain range near Chikkamagaluru known for dramatic landscapes, winding roads and spiritual significance. It can be paired with selected nearby viewpoints and hill attractions.

Fog and rain can reduce visibility on the mountain roads. Allow sufficient time and follow local traffic guidance.

Hills and heritage
14. Kemmanagundi

Kemmanagundi is a hill station surrounded by gardens, forested hills and scenic viewpoints. It is an appealing destination for travellers who prefer cooler weather and relaxed sightseeing.

The Z Point walking route and other local attractions require time on foot and may be affected by weather.

Hill station
15. Hebbe Falls

Hebbe Falls is a beautiful waterfall in the Chikkamagaluru region, surrounded by forest and coffee-growing landscapes. It is a popular choice for visitors interested in natural scenery.

Access arrangements may involve authorised local transport or walking, depending on current rules. Check the official access conditions before including it in a taxi itinerary.

Waterfall and forest
16. Coorg and Madikeri

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is celebrated for coffee plantations, forested hills and scenic countryside. Madikeri is a useful base for exploring the region's viewpoints, heritage attractions and nearby nature spots.

Coorg is a large region, so select a manageable group of attractions based on your hotel and travel dates.

Coffee country
17. Abbey Falls

Abbey Falls near Madikeri is a popular waterfall surrounded by greenery and plantation landscapes. A designated access route leads visitors towards the viewing area.

Entry and viewpoint access may be restricted during unsafe conditions. Follow the marked route and do not cross barriers.

Waterfall
18. Raja's Seat, Madikeri

Raja's Seat is a well-known garden and viewpoint overlooking the hills around Madikeri. It is a convenient stop for enjoying the landscape, especially around sunrise or sunset when conditions are favourable.

This attraction can be included in a Coorg sightseeing route with other nearby town landmarks.

Viewpoint and garden
19. Sakleshpur

Sakleshpur is a hill region known for plantations, green valleys and mountain scenery. It offers a relaxed base for countryside drives and selected local heritage or nature stops.

Combine it with nearby destinations such as Manjarabad Fort and Bisle Ghat only after checking distances and road conditions.

Plantations and hills
20. Manjarabad Fort

Manjarabad Fort is a distinctive star-shaped historic fort near Sakleshpur. Its elevated setting and unusual layout make it an interesting stop for history enthusiasts and landscape photographers.

The visit involves walking around the fort area. Check opening and access arrangements before travelling.

Historic fort
21. Yana Rock Formations

Yana is famous for its striking dark rock formations rising above a green forest landscape. The site is a distinctive geological and nature destination in the Uttara Kannada region.

Visiting involves walking through a natural setting, and the destination may require a separate route from central Western Ghats hill stations.

Rock formations and forest
22. Wayanad Hill Country

Wayanad, in Kerala, offers a mix of misty hills, forest landscapes, lakes, waterfalls, caves and heritage attractions. It can form a separate multi-day extension to a Western Ghats tour.

Since Wayanad is spread across a large area, plan its attractions together by region rather than attempting to cover the entire district in a single day.

Kerala hill country
23. Gangamoola Region

Gangamoola is a forested mountain region associated with the origins of the Tunga, Bhadra and Netravati river systems. The surrounding landscape highlights the ecological importance of the Western Ghats.

Access is governed by forest and conservation regulations. Do not assume that all areas are open to vehicles or independent visitors.

Forest and river origins
24. Sirimane Falls

Sirimane Falls near the Kigga and Sringeri region is a scenic waterfall surrounded by greenery. It can be considered for a regional itinerary combining nature with nearby pilgrimage destinations.

Water conditions, local entry arrangements and weather determine whether a visit is suitable on your travel date.

Waterfall and countryside
25. Horanadu Annapoorneshwari Temple

Horanadu is a significant pilgrimage destination in the Malnad region, known for Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple amid green hills and scenic roads.

It can be combined with Kalasa and selected nearby destinations, allowing sufficient time for the mountain drive and temple visit.

Temple and mountain scenery
Route planning: These destinations are spread across several districts. A practical tour should focus on one region or a connected route at a time. Trekking permissions, forest entry, waterfall access and road conditions must be confirmed before travel.

Travel smart

Important Western Ghats Travel Tips

Check mountain road conditions

Fog, heavy rain, landslides and fallen trees may affect ghat roads. Check current conditions before setting out.

Choose the right season

The monsoon creates lush scenery but may limit waterfalls, viewpoints and treks. Check local access advice for your dates.

Confirm trekking permissions

Protected areas and designated peaks may require permits, authorised guides or advance reservations.

Allow enough driving time

Winding roads and widely separated destinations make realistic daily planning important.

Respect wildlife and forests

Do not litter, feed wildlife, enter restricted trails or disturb forest habitats.

Confirm the taxi quotation

Discuss dates, passenger count, vehicle category, route, overnight stays and applicable extra charges before booking.

Can we visit every attraction in this list?

Are trekking and forest visits included automatically?

Yes. The route can be adapted for family sightseeing by selecting suitable viewpoints, towns, temples and accessible attractions. Some treks and difficult trails may not suit every traveller.
