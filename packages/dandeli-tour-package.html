Suggested Travel Plan

Dandeli Tour Itinerary

A sample three-day itinerary from Mangaluru. The route can be adjusted to your travel dates, activity bookings and preferred sightseeing stops.

Day 1

Mangaluru to Dandeli

Begin your journey from Mangaluru and travel towards Dandeli. After arrival, check in to your independently arranged stay. Depending on arrival time, enjoy a relaxed local outing or a pre-booked river activity.

Day 2

River Adventures and Wildlife

Choose an available Kali River activity such as rafting or kayaking through an authorised operator. Depending on safari availability, include a guided wildlife experience or birdwatching. Allow time for activity briefings and transfers.

Day 3

Forest Attractions and Return

If access and permissions allow, visit a selected attraction such as Syntheri Rocks, Kavala Caves or a scenic viewpoint. Start the return journey after sightseeing, allowing enough time for the road trip to your agreed drop location.

Optional Extension

Waterfalls and Nearby Destinations

Add another day if you want to include distant destinations such as Sathodi Falls or Magod Falls. These attractions may require substantial additional driving, so plan them as a separate sightseeing day where practical.

Important: Dandeli's attractions are spread across forest areas, and some sites have permit or access restrictions. Rafting, safaris, cave visits and other activities are not guaranteed and may be unavailable due to weather, water levels or official restrictions. Before You Travel

Useful Tips for Your Dandeli Trip

Plan Your Adventure

  • Check river activity availability and operating conditions before travel.
  • Use authorised operators for rafting, kayaking and other adventure activities.
  • Follow the safety briefing and wear the required safety equipment.
  • Confirm forest entry permissions, cave access and safari arrangements in advance.
  • Carry suitable walking shoes, drinking water and rain protection.
  • Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed; maintain a safe distance from wild animals.
  • Allow extra travel time on forest roads and rural routes.

Choose a Suitable Taxi
