Places to Visit in Mysore

Tap or click any attraction below to read about its highlights and plan your visit. You can select the places that interest you most when enquiring about your taxi package.

1. Mysuru Palace (Amba Vilas Palace)

Mysuru Palace is the city's most recognised royal landmark and a highlight of almost every Mysuru sightseeing trip. Its Indo-Saracenic architecture features grand domes, ornate halls, decorative doors, colourful interiors and royal collections.

What to see: Palace architecture, Durbar Hall, Kalyana Mantapa and the palace complex.

Suggested time: 1.5–2.5 hours, depending on crowds and your interest.

Planning tip: Check current visiting hours, ticket arrangements and photography rules before your visit. Illumination schedules can vary.

Royal Heritage Must Visit
2. Chamundeshwari Temple and Chamundi Hills

Located on Chamundi Hills, this important temple is dedicated to Goddess Chamundeshwari, the presiding deity associated with Mysuru. The hilltop is also known for its views across the city and its religious significance.

What to see: The temple, traditional sculptures, hill scenery and viewpoints where accessible.

Suggested time: 1.5–2.5 hours, allowing extra time during busy temple periods.

Planning tip: Temple queues and hill traffic can affect the schedule. Follow local parking and visitor instructions.

Temple Hill Views
3. Nandi Statue on Chamundi Hill

The large monolithic Nandi, the sacred bull associated with Lord Shiva, is a well-known stop along the Chamundi Hills route. Its carved stone form makes it an interesting place for visitors interested in religious art and traditional craftsmanship.

What to see: The monumental Nandi sculpture and surrounding hill setting.

Suggested time: 20–40 minutes, depending on access and crowds.

Planning tip: Coordinate this stop with Chamundeshwari Temple to avoid unnecessary driving.

Heritage Photography
4. Mysuru Zoo

Sri Chamarajendra Zoological Gardens, commonly known as Mysuru Zoo, is a popular family attraction with a variety of animal habitats. It is a good option for families and travellers who enjoy wildlife and outdoor walks.

What to see: Animal exhibits, landscaped paths and educational displays.

Suggested time: 2–3 hours or longer for a leisurely visit.

Planning tip: Confirm the zoo's opening day, timings, ticket rules and visitor guidelines before departure.

Family Friendly Wildlife
5. St. Philomena's Church

St. Philomena's Church is a striking historic church recognised for its Gothic-inspired architecture, tall twin spires and stained-glass windows. Its distinctive design makes it a popular heritage and architecture stop in Mysuru.

What to see: Exterior architecture, spires and stained-glass details where visitor access is permitted.

Suggested time: 30–45 minutes.

Planning tip: Respect worship services, dress appropriately and follow photography restrictions.

Architecture Heritage
6. Brindavan Gardens and KRS Dam

Brindavan Gardens, near the Krishna Raja Sagara Dam, is known for its landscaped terraces, ornamental plants and garden pathways. It is often included in an evening sightseeing plan, subject to opening hours and the day's visitor arrangements.

What to see: Terraced gardens, landscaped spaces and fountain displays when operating.

Suggested time: 1.5–2.5 hours, plus travel time from Mysuru city.

Planning tip: This is outside the city centre. Check current garden timings, fountain schedules and access arrangements before setting out.

Gardens Evening Visit
7. Jaganmohan Palace Art Gallery

Jaganmohan Palace is a historic palace that houses an art gallery and cultural collections. It is a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Indian art, Mysuru's royal history and traditional painting styles.

What to see: Art displays, paintings, sculptures and historic architecture, subject to gallery access.

Suggested time: 45–90 minutes.

Planning tip: Check current gallery opening hours and any temporary closures.

Art and Culture History
8. Devaraja Market

Devaraja Market is a traditional Mysuru market filled with colourful flower stalls, produce, spices and everyday local activity. It offers a glimpse of the city's shopping culture and is a good place to experience local life.

What to see: Flower stalls, produce, local goods and traditional market scenes.

Suggested time: 30–60 minutes.

Planning tip: Markets can be crowded. Keep valuables secure and use a nearby designated parking area.

Local Culture Shopping
9. Karanji Lake and Nature Park

Karanji Lake offers a calmer outdoor experience with lake scenery and nature-focused visitor areas. Depending on the facilities open on the day, visitors may enjoy birdwatching and a relaxed walk away from the busy city centre.

What to see: Lake views, birdlife and designated nature-park facilities.

Suggested time: 45–90 minutes.

Planning tip: Confirm the current entry arrangements, available facilities and opening hours.

Nature Relaxing Stop
10. Mysuru Sand Sculpture Museum

The Sand Sculpture Museum showcases detailed artworks formed from sand, featuring subjects inspired by culture, mythology, wildlife and popular themes. It is a compact attraction that can be added to a city sightseeing route.

What to see: Themed sand sculptures and intricate artistic designs.

Suggested time: 30–60 minutes.

Planning tip: Confirm current opening hours and access before including it in your schedule.

Art Family Visit
11. Railway Museum

The Mysuru Railway Museum introduces visitors to railway history through historic railway equipment, locomotives and exhibits. It can be an enjoyable additional stop for families, children and people interested in trains.

What to see: Historic railway exhibits and preserved railway equipment.

Suggested time: 45–75 minutes.

Planning tip: Verify opening hours and visitor access before visiting.

Museum Family Friendly
12. Lalitha Mahal Palace View

Lalitha Mahal is an elegant palace building near the foothills of Chamundi Hills, known for its grand architectural appearance. It adds another perspective to Mysuru's royal history.

What to see: The palace's exterior and architectural setting where access is permitted.

Suggested time: 20–40 minutes for an exterior stop, subject to access.

Planning tip: Some palace facilities may operate as hospitality premises. Do not assume that all interior areas are open to sightseeing visitors.

Royal Architecture Photo Stop
13. Srirangapatna Heritage Tour

Srirangapatna, near Mysuru, is an important historic town associated with Tipu Sultan and the history of the Kingdom of Mysore. Depending on your schedule, selected monuments can be visited as an additional excursion.

Possible stops: Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple, Dariya Daulat Bagh, Gumbaz and selected historic sites, subject to opening and access.

Suggested time: 2–4 hours for selected stops, excluding longer visits.

Planning tip: This is a separate excursion from central Mysuru. Choose a few places rather than trying to cover every monument in one short visit.

History Nearby Excursion
14. Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary

Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary is a nature destination on the Cauvery River near Srirangapatna. It is known for birdlife, riverside scenery and boat-based wildlife viewing when operating conditions allow.

What to see: Birds, river islands and natural scenery.

Suggested time: 1–2 hours, plus travel time.

Planning tip: Boat services, entry and wildlife viewing depend on operating conditions. Check current visitor arrangements before travel.

Birdwatching Nature
15. Somanathapura Chennakesava Temple

Somanathapura is known for its historic Hoysala temple architecture and intricate stone carvings. It is a strong choice for visitors who enjoy heritage monuments and detailed traditional craftsmanship.

What to see: Ornate stone carvings, sculpted walls and historic temple architecture.

Suggested time: 60–90 minutes, excluding travel time.

Planning tip: This is an additional excursion outside the main city sightseeing circuit. Check current monument access and local visitor guidance.

Hoysala Heritage Architecture
16. Mysuru Local Food and Shopping

Complete your Mysuru experience with local food and shopping. Mysuru is associated with Mysore Pak, traditional silk, sandalwood products and local handicrafts.

Ideas: Sample regional dishes, purchase local sweets and explore established shops or traditional markets.

Suggested time: 30–90 minutes depending on your interests.

Planning tip: Shopping is optional and can be fitted around your sightseeing schedule.

Local Food Shopping
Suggested Travel Plan

2-Day Mysore Tour Itinerary

This is a suggested plan, not a fixed schedule. The final route can be adjusted to your pickup location, travel dates, opening hours and preferred attractions.

DAY 1

Royal Mysuru City Tour

  • Morning: Depart from Mangalore according to your booking and travel plan, or begin sightseeing if already staying in Mysuru.
  • Late morning: Visit Mysuru Palace.
  • Afternoon: Explore selected city attractions such as St. Philomena's Church and Jaganmohan Palace Art Gallery.
  • Late afternoon: Visit Devaraja Market or the Railway Museum if time permits.
  • Evening: Enjoy dinner and an overnight stay in Mysuru.
DAY 2

Chamundi Hills and Gardens

  • Morning: Visit Chamundeshwari Temple and Nandi Statue, allowing time for queues.
  • Late morning: Choose Mysuru Zoo, Karanji Lake or the Sand Sculpture Museum.
  • Afternoon: Have lunch and enjoy any remaining city sightseeing.
  • Late afternoon: Travel towards Brindavan Gardens if opening times and your route permit.
  • Evening: Return according to your agreed travel plan.
Planning a trip from Mangalore? Mangalore to Mysuru is a substantial road journey. For a more relaxed experience, consider a three-day plan with an overnight stay and enough time for the return drive. Brindavan Gardens, Srirangapatna, Ranganathittu and Somanathapura are best treated as optional stops rather than trying to cover every attraction in two days.

Mysore Tour Package FAQs

Useful information to help you plan your Mysuru sightseeing trip.

How many days are needed for a Mysore tour?

Two days can cover selected city highlights. Three days are more comfortable for travellers who also want to visit nearby destinations such as Srirangapatna, Ranganathittu or Somanathapura.

Can I book a Mysore tour taxi from Mangalore?

Yes. Contact Radhakrishna Taxi Service to discuss your pickup point, travel dates, number of passengers, vehicle preference and itinerary. The route and booking details can then be confirmed with you.

Which places are best for a family trip?

Mysuru Palace, Mysuru Zoo, Brindavan Gardens, the Railway Museum and selected city attractions are popular options for families. Choose stops according to the ages and interests of your group.

Can we customise the sightseeing itinerary?

Yes. You can discuss the attractions you want to visit and the duration of your trip before confirming your taxi booking. The final schedule should allow for driving time, queues and opening hours.

What is included in the taxi package price?

The price depends on the vehicle, travel dates, number of days, distance, route and itinerary. Ask for a quotation that clearly explains applicable inclusions, driver allowance, parking, tolls, waiting time and any other charges before confirming.

Can we visit Brindavan Gardens and Srirangapatna on the same trip?

Yes, they can be considered as part of a wider Mysuru itinerary. The practical schedule depends on your available time, chosen attractions and current opening arrangements. A longer trip is usually more comfortable.

What is the best time to visit Mysuru?

Mysuru can be visited throughout the year. Weather, festivals, crowds and personal preferences affect the experience. During Dasara and other busy periods, plan transport and accommodation in advance.

How do I enquire about the Mysore tour package?
