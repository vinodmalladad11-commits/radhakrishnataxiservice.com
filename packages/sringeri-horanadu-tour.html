Temples and Places to Visit

Click or tap any attraction to expand its details. The itinerary can be customised according to your preferred temples, travel dates, darshan schedule and available time.

1. Sri Sharadamba Temple, Sringeri

Sri Sharadamba Temple is the principal pilgrimage destination in Sringeri and is dedicated to Goddess Sharadamba, associated with learning, wisdom and knowledge. Devotees visit to offer prayers and receive blessings.

What to experience: Darshan, prayer and the sacred atmosphere of the temple complex.

Suggested time: Allow 1–2 hours or longer during crowded periods.

Planning tip: Follow the current dress code and temple instructions. Check official notices for darshan arrangements before travelling.

Main Pilgrimage Temple Darshan
2. Vidyashankara Temple, Sringeri

Vidyashankara Temple is renowned for its distinctive blend of Hoysala and Dravidian architectural features. The stone structure and its sculpted zodiac pillars make it an important place for devotees, architecture enthusiasts and history lovers.

What to see: Stone carvings, temple architecture and the twelve zodiac-associated pillars.

Suggested time: 30–60 minutes.

Planning tip: Visit respectfully and follow the temple's current access and photography rules.

Architecture Heritage
3. Sri Sringeri Sharada Peetham

Sringeri Sharada Peetham is an important traditional centre associated with Adi Shankaracharya and the Advaita Vedanta tradition. Its spiritual heritage has made Sringeri a major destination for pilgrims from across India.

What to experience: Explore the permitted temple and religious areas and learn about the institution's spiritual heritage.

Suggested time: Depends on darshan and the areas open to visitors.

Planning tip: Guru darshan and other activities may have separate schedules and restrictions. Check the official Sringeri Sharada Peetham website before your visit.

Spiritual Heritage Pilgrimage
4. Tunga River and Sringeri Riverfront

The Tunga River flows beside Sringeri's temple area and forms an important part of the town's spiritual setting. Visitors often spend quiet time by the river and observe traditional religious activities.

What to experience: Riverside scenery, peaceful surroundings and views of the temple area.

Suggested time: 20–45 minutes.

Planning tip: Follow local instructions near the water. River levels and currents can change, especially during heavy rainfall.

Riverfront Scenic Stop
5. Sri Torana Ganapati Temple, Sringeri

Torana Ganapati is among the religious stops visitors may include when exploring Sringeri's temple surroundings. Devotees visit Lord Ganesha for blessings and to begin their spiritual journey with prayer.

What to experience: A short temple visit and prayer, subject to access arrangements.

Suggested time: 15–30 minutes.

Planning tip: Temple access and customary practices should be confirmed locally.

Temple Devotional Visit
6. Sri Malahanikareshwara Temple

Sri Malahanikareshwara Temple is a traditional Shiva temple associated with Sringeri's religious heritage. It offers another opportunity for devotees to visit a sacred site while exploring the town.

What to experience: Darshan and traditional temple architecture.

Suggested time: 20–40 minutes.

Planning tip: Coordinate the visit with your main Sringeri temple schedule to reduce unnecessary driving.

Shiva Temple Heritage
7. Sri Rishyasringeshwara Temple, Kigga

Kigga is a village near Sringeri known for the Rishyasringeshwara Temple. Dedicated to Sage Rishyasringa, the temple is a popular addition for pilgrims interested in the region's traditional religious history.

What to experience: Temple darshan, village surroundings and a peaceful rural setting.

Suggested time: 30–60 minutes, excluding travel.

Planning tip: Kigga is outside central Sringeri, so allow time for the local road journey.

Nearby Temple Village Visit
8. Sirimane Waterfalls

Sirimane Waterfalls, in the wider Kigga area, is a scenic waterfall surrounded by the greenery of the Western Ghats. It can be a refreshing optional stop for travellers who want to combine their temple visit with nature.

What to see: Waterfall scenery and the surrounding green landscape from permitted visitor areas.

Suggested time: 45–90 minutes, excluding driving time.

Planning tip: Water flow and access can vary with the season and weather. Check local conditions and follow safety instructions.

Waterfall Nature
9. Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple, Horanadu

Horanadu is home to the revered Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple, dedicated to the Goddess of nourishment. The temple is set amid the green hills of the Malnad region and is an important destination for pilgrims.

What to experience: Darshan of Goddess Annapoorneshwari, prayer and the temple's devotional atmosphere.

Suggested time: Allow 1–2 hours or more during busy days.

Planning tip: Follow the temple's current dress code and visitor instructions. Check darshan timings and special festival arrangements before travel.

Main Pilgrimage Temple Darshan
10. Horanadu Temple Annadanam

Horanadu Temple is known for offering annaprasadam to visitors. The meal tradition is an important part of the temple's hospitality and the area's pilgrimage experience.

What to experience: Temple-provided meals when available under the temple's current arrangements.

Suggested time: Allow additional time around meal service and darshan.

Planning tip: Follow the temple's meal timings and queue instructions. Meal arrangements may be adjusted on special days.

Temple Tradition Pilgrim Facilities
11. Sri Kalaseshwara Temple, Kalasa

Kalasa is a historic temple town in the Malnad region. Sri Kalaseshwara Temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and is commonly included in pilgrimage routes linking Sringeri and Horanadu.

What to experience: Temple darshan, religious architecture and the surroundings of Kalasa town.

Suggested time: 30–60 minutes.

Planning tip: Kalasa is near Horanadu, so the two places can often be planned together, depending on traffic and temple timings.

Shiva Temple Nearby Pilgrimage
12. Bhadra River and Kalasa Riverside

The Bhadra River and its surrounding landscapes contribute to the natural beauty of the Kalasa and Horanadu region. Visitors may enjoy peaceful views of the river from accessible and permitted locations.

What to see: Riverside scenery, forested hills and the green Malnad landscape.

Suggested time: 15–40 minutes for a short stop.

Planning tip: Do not enter the river or restricted banks without checking local safety conditions. Water levels can rise quickly during heavy rain.

Nature Scenic Stop
13. Hariharapura and Temple Surroundings

Hariharapura is a peaceful religious destination in the wider Malnad region, associated with traditional spiritual institutions and temple visits. It can be considered as an additional stop when your schedule and route allow.

What to experience: Selected temples, religious surroundings and rural scenery, depending on local access.

Suggested time: 45–90 minutes if included in the route.

Planning tip: This is an optional extension. Confirm the exact places you want to visit before setting the driving route.

Spiritual Heritage Optional Excursion
14. Kudremukh Scenic Region

The Kudremukh region is known for its green mountain landscapes, grasslands and forested Western Ghats scenery. It is a nature excursion rather than a simple stop within the main temple circuit.

What to experience: Scenic drives and permitted viewpoints in the region.

Suggested time: Allow several additional hours, depending on the route and authorised activities.

Planning tip: Protected-area access, trekking and vehicle permissions may be restricted. Check current rules before including this destination; do not assume that all forest roads are open to visitors.

Western Ghats Optional Extension
15. Gangamoola Region — Optional Nature Visit

Gangamoola is associated with the origins of several rivers in the Western Ghats and lies within a sensitive natural landscape. It may interest travellers looking for a deeper nature experience beyond the principal temple destinations.

What to experience: The wider forest and mountain landscape only through authorised access and permitted activities.

Suggested time: Depends on current permissions, route and activity availability.

Planning tip: This is not a guaranteed roadside sightseeing stop. Check forest permissions, local access and safety restrictions before planning any visit.

Nature Access Restrictions May Apply
16. Scenic Malnad Drive and Local Stops

The journey between Sringeri, Kalasa and Horanadu passes through the scenic Malnad region, with green hills, plantations, winding roads and forested landscapes.

What to experience: Countryside scenery and brief photo stops at safe, permitted roadside locations.

Suggested time: Allow for road conditions and any agreed stops along the route.

Planning tip: Mountain roads can be narrow and winding. Use designated stopping areas and avoid stopping in unsafe places or restricted forest zones.

Scenic Drive Malnad Region
Suggested Travel Plan

2-Day Sringeri Horanadu Tour Itinerary

A sample schedule for a pilgrimage covering the principal temples and selected nearby places. The final route depends on your pickup point, darshan timings, road conditions and preferred stops.

DAY 1

Sringeri Temple Darshan

  • Morning: Start from your agreed pickup location and travel towards Sringeri.
  • Midday: Visit Sri Sharadamba Temple, subject to arrival time and darshan arrangements.
  • Afternoon: Explore Vidyashankara Temple and selected nearby religious sites.
  • Evening: Spend time near the Tunga River where permitted and check in to your accommodation.
  • Optional: Visit Kigga if sufficient daylight and time remain.
DAY 2

Kalasa and Horanadu Pilgrimage

  • Morning: Travel towards Kalasa and visit Sri Kalaseshwara Temple if included in your plan.
  • Late morning: Continue to Horanadu and visit Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple.
  • Midday: Allow time for darshan and temple meal arrangements where available.
  • Afternoon: Enjoy selected permitted scenic stops around the Malnad region.
  • Evening: Begin your return journey according to the agreed route and travel time.
Travel planning tip: A two-day tour is suitable for the main pilgrimage route when the pickup location and road timings permit. Add a third day if you want to include Kigga, Sirimane Waterfalls, Hariharapura or other optional attractions without rushing temple visits. Darshan schedules and temple arrangements can change, so verify them before departure.

Sringeri Horanadu Tour FAQs

Useful answers for planning your temple tour and taxi booking.

How many days are needed for Sringeri and Horanadu?

Two days can cover the principal temples if the journey is planned carefully. Three days gives you more time for Kigga, Sirimane Waterfalls, Kalasa and optional nearby attractions.

Can I book a taxi for Sringeri and Horanadu from Mangalore?

Contact Radhakrishna Taxi Service with your pickup location, travel dates, passenger count and preferred itinerary to discuss a suitable private taxi arrangement and quotation.

Which temples are included in this tour?

The main itinerary focuses on Sri Sharadamba Temple and Vidyashankara Temple in Sringeri, and Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple in Horanadu. Kalaseshwara Temple in Kalasa and Rishyasringeshwara Temple in Kigga are optional additions.

Is there a dress code at Sringeri Sharadamba Temple?

The Sringeri Sharada Peetham publishes dress and visitor rules for temple darshan. Check the official temple website before your visit and follow the current instructions to avoid difficulties at entry.

Is food available at Horanadu Temple?

The temple is known for providing annaprasadam to visitors. Follow the current temple meal timings and instructions, especially during festivals and busy pilgrimage periods.

Can we visit Sirimane Waterfalls during the temple tour?

Yes, it can be considered as an optional visit near Kigga. Allow extra travel time and confirm current access, weather conditions and safety guidance before adding it to your route.

Can Kudremukh or Gangamoola be included?

These are optional nature excursions and may have protected-area restrictions or require specific permissions. Confirm current access and authorised activities before including either in your itinerary.

What is included in the taxi package price?

The quotation depends on your vehicle, travel dates, number of days, route and sightseeing plan. Confirm whether driver allowance, tolls, parking, applicable state charges, waiting time and other expenses are included or payable separately.

How do I book the Sringeri Horanadu tour?

Contact Radhakrishna Taxi Service by phone, WhatsApp or email. Share your travel date, pickup point, passenger count, trip duration and the temples you want to visit so that a suitable quotation can be discussed.
