Suggested Travel Plan

Coastal Karnataka Temple Tour Itinerary

A sample four-day route. The final schedule depends on your pickup location, temple priorities, darshan queues and travel dates.

Day 1

Mangaluru and Kateel Temple Circuit

Begin with pickup from Mangaluru or your agreed location. Visit Kateel Durga Parameshwari Temple and selected temples in Mangaluru, such as Mangaladevi, Kudroli Gokarnanatheshwara and Kadri Manjunatha. Overnight stay can be arranged independently in Mangaluru.

Day 2

Udupi Sri Krishna Matha and Nearby Temples

Travel towards Udupi and visit Sri Krishna Matha, Anantheshwara Temple and Chandramouleshwara Temple. If time permits, add another selected temple around the Udupi or Kundapura region.

Day 3

Kollur Mookambika and Murudeshwar

Proceed to Kollur for darshan at Mookambika Temple. Continue towards Murudeshwar to visit the Shiva Temple and statue. Travel times and temple queues should be considered when planning this day.

Day 4

Gokarna Pilgrimage and Return

For a longer north-coast circuit, visit Gokarna Mahabaleshwar Temple and Maha Ganapati Temple. Finish the trip with a return journey or a drop at a pre-agreed destination. An additional day may be more comfortable for a Mangaluru return.

Important planning note: This is a sample route, not a fixed schedule. Covering Mangaluru, Dharmasthala, Kukke Subramanya, Udupi, Kollur, Murudeshwar and Gokarna together may require additional days. Tell us your priority temples so we can plan a practical itinerary.
Before You Travel

Useful Tips for Your Temple Tour

Plan a Comfortable Pilgrimage

  • Check temple opening hours and special darshan schedules before departure.
  • Wear modest clothing and follow the individual temple's dress code.
  • Allow additional time for queues, pujas and religious ceremonies.
  • Confirm whether photography is permitted inside temple premises.
  • Keep essential medicines, drinking water and personal items handy.
  • Book accommodation in advance during festivals and busy pilgrimage periods.

Choose a Suitable Vehicle

Select your vehicle based on the number of travellers, luggage and comfort requirements.

  • Sedan: Suitable for smaller groups.
  • Ertiga: A practical option for families.
  • Innova or Crysta: More space and comfort for longer journeys.
  • Tempo Traveller: Suitable for larger groups, subject to availability.

Contact us to confirm vehicle availability and the quotation for your planned route.
