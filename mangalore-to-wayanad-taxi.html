Popular Wayanad Places to Visit

Wayanad has many natural, cultural and sightseeing attractions. Depending on your route and available time, your taxi itinerary can include places such as:

Edakkal Caves – Famous prehistoric rock shelters near Ambalavayal.
Banasura Sagar Dam – Scenic reservoir surrounded by Western Ghats.
Pookode Lake – Popular freshwater lake near Vythiri.
Soochipara Falls – Scenic waterfall near Meppadi.
Kuruva Island – Natural island area on the Kabini River.
Chembra Peak – Popular Wayanad mountain and trekking destination.
Lakkidi View Point – Scenic viewpoint overlooking the Western Ghats.
Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary – Forest and wildlife destination around Wayanad.
Vythiri – Green hill destination surrounded by forests.
Kalpetta – Major town and convenient base for Wayanad travel.
