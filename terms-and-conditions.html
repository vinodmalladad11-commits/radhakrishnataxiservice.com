3. How Cancellation Charges Are Calculated

  • Cancellation charges are calculated as a percentage of the total confirmed trip fare.
  • The calculation is not based only on the advance payment.
  • Our usual booking advance is 10% of the total trip fare.
  • If the customer has paid more than the cancellation charge, the eligible remaining amount will be refunded.
  • If the full fare has been paid in advance, the applicable cancellation charge will still be calculated using the same percentage.
Example:
Total trip fare: ₹5,000
Amount paid: ₹2,000
Cancellation charge at 5%: ₹250
Eligible refund: ₹1,750

4. Flight or Train Cancellation

If a customer cannot reach the scheduled pickup location because their flight or train has been cancelled:

  • A full refund will be provided if the cancellation is reported at least 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time.
  • Valid flight or train cancellation proof must be submitted.
  • This special full-refund provision applies irrespective of the regular cancellation schedule.

5. Late Arrival and Waiting Charges

If a customer is delayed at the pickup location, we will make reasonable efforts to provide the booked taxi service, subject to vehicle and driver availability.

Additional waiting charges may apply depending on the waiting time and applicable booking conditions.

6. Refund Processing Time

Approved eligible refunds will be processed within 2 working days after cancellation approval.

7. Booking & Confirmation

  • All bookings are subject to vehicle and driver availability.
  • A booking is confirmed only after receiving confirmation from Radhakrishna Taxi Service.
  • Customers should provide accurate pickup locations, destinations, travel dates, contact numbers and passenger details.
  • Any changes to the booking must be communicated in advance and are subject to availability.

8. Advance Payment & Fare

  • Customers may be required to pay an advance to confirm their booking.
  • The normal advance payment is 10% of the confirmed trip fare, unless otherwise communicated.
  • The remaining balance must be paid as agreed during booking confirmation.
  • The final fare will be based on the confirmed trip details and applicable booking terms.

9. Additional Expenses

Tolls, parking charges and other applicable trip-related expenses are additional unless specifically included in the confirmed quotation.

10. Passenger Responsibilities

  • Passengers must be present at the agreed pickup location and time.
  • Customers should provide correct contact details and remain reachable during pickup.
  • Passengers must maintain proper conduct and respect the driver and vehicle.
  • Passengers are responsible for their personal belongings and luggage.
  • Any damage caused to the vehicle due to passenger negligence may result in applicable recovery charges.

11. Delays & Unforeseen Circumstances

Traffic congestion, road closures, adverse weather, government restrictions, vehicle breakdowns and other unforeseen circumstances may affect travel schedules.

We will make reasonable efforts to communicate significant delays and arrange suitable alternatives where possible.

12. Changes to Terms & Conditions

Radhakrishna Taxi Service reserves the right to update these Terms & Conditions when required. The applicable booking conditions will be communicated to customers at the time of confirmation.
