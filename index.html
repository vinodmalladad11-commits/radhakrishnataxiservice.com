Destination directory

Every stop has
a story.

Tap a destination for a quick idea of what to see there. We can combine any of these stops into your custom taxi tour.

Mangalore

Mangalore is the gateway to coastal Karnataka— known for beaches, seafood, temples and the distinctive culture of the Konkan coast. Visit Panambur or Tannirbhavi Beach, Kudroli Gokarnath Temple, Mangaladevi Temple and Pilikula.

Udupi, Malpe & St. Mary’s Island

Udupi combines the Sri Krishna Temple, famous vegetarian cuisine and a relaxed coastal atmosphere. Nearby Malpe Beach is the usual starting point for a boat trip to St. Mary’s Island.

Dharmasthala, Kukke Subramanya & Kollur

These are major pilgrimage destinations reached through scenic Western Ghats roads. Dharmasthala is known for Manjunatha Temple, Kukke for Subramanya Temple, and Kollur for Mookambika Temple.

Kundapura & Maravanthe

This is one of Karnataka’s scenic coastal drives. Maravanthe is known for the unusual view of beach on one side of the road and backwaters on the other.

Murudeshwar

Murudeshwar is a sea-facing temple town with a large Shiva statue, tall temple tower and beach.

Gokarna

Gokarna blends a pilgrimage-town atmosphere with beaches such as Om, Kudle, Half Moon and Paradise.

Karwar

Karwar offers a quieter stretch of the Konkan coast, with beaches, islands, sea views and easy access to nearby Goa.

Coorg

Coorg, also called Kodagu, is a hill district of coffee estates, misty roads and waterfalls.

Chikmagalur & Kudremukh

Chikmagalur is a coffee-country escape with mountain roads, viewpoints and waterfalls. Kudremukh adds grasslands, forest landscapes and trekking opportunities.

Dandeli

Dandeli is a forest and river destination in Uttara Kannada. It is popular for nature stays, Kali River activities, wildlife and adventurous group trips.

Jog Falls & Shivamogga

Jog Falls is one of the state’s signature waterfall destinations, best combined with the green landscapes around Shivamogga.

Mysuru & Srirangapatna

Mysuru is celebrated for Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hill, markets and gardens. Nearby Srirangapatna adds river-island history, forts and monuments.

Belur, Halebidu & Shravanabelagola

This heritage circuit is prized for detailed Hoysala temple carvings at Belur and Halebidu, plus the towering Bahubali statue.

Hampi

Hampi is an extraordinary landscape of boulders, temples and ruins from the Vijayanagara Empire.

Badami, Aihole & Pattadakal

This classic North Karnataka circuit traces early temple architecture including Badami’s rock-cut caves, Aihole and Pattadakal.

Vijayapura

Vijayapura, formerly Bijapur, is known for the monumental Gol Gumbaz and Deccan Sultanate-era monuments.

Bidar & Kalaburagi

Bidar features a major fort and Bahmani-era monuments. Kalaburagi offers more Deccan heritage.

Bengaluru & Nandi Hills

Bengaluru offers city attractions, parks, palaces, museums and food experiences. Nandi Hills is a popular nearby hilltop drive.

Bandipur & Nagarhole

These are two important wildlife landscapes in southern Karnataka, often chosen for forest stays and guided safari experiences.

Chitradurga

Chitradurga is famous for its dramatic hill fort, boulder-strewn terrain and regional history.
