Mangalore to Ooty Travel Route

The Mangalore to Ooty journey is a long-distance road trip connecting coastal Karnataka with the Nilgiri hills of Tamil Nadu. The total road distance is approximately 400 to 430 km depending on the selected route and exact pickup and destination locations.

Journey time can vary significantly because of traffic, road conditions, weather, stops and the selected route. Travellers should plan sufficient time for the journey.

Ooty can also be included in a longer South India itinerary covering Mysore, Coorg, Wayanad, Coonoor and other destinations.
