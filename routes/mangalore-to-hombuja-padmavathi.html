Plan Your Hombuja Padmavathi Temple Visit

Hombuja, also known as Humcha, is a historic Jain pilgrimage centre in Karnataka associated with the Padmavathi temple. Visitors can plan a devotional trip with time for temple visits and nearby local attractions.

Before travelling, verify the temple's current timings, special rituals and visitor arrangements. Send us your preferred date, pickup point and passenger count so we can discuss the journey and provide a quotation.

Trip Planning

  • Padmavathi temple visit at Hombuja
  • Time for darshan and prayers
  • Family and group pilgrimage
  • Planned stops along the route, where practical
  • Return journey arrangements

Temple access and schedules may vary on festival days or during special events.
