Travel from Mangalore to Velankanni

Velankanni is a well-known pilgrimage destination in Tamil Nadu, visited by travellers throughout the year. A private taxi gives your group flexibility to plan the departure time, luggage and journey breaks. As this is a long-distance trip, allow adequate travel time and confirm the itinerary before departure.

When enquiring, provide your travel date, pickup point, number of passengers, preferred vehicle and return plan. Any additional destinations, waiting time or sightseeing stops should be discussed before the booking is confirmed.
