Taxi from Mangalore to Kukke Sri Subrahmanya Temple

Plan your temple visit with a pickup time and vehicle that suit your schedule and group size.

Kukke Subrahmanya, located in Dakshina Kannada district, is a well-known pilgrimage destination near the foothills of Kumara Parvatha. The temple is dedicated to Lord Subrahmanya and is associated with traditional religious rituals and worship.

A taxi from Mangalore lets you plan your departure and temple visit around your own schedule. The road journey is approximately 104–105 km and commonly takes around 2.5–3 hours, although traffic, weather, stops and road conditions can affect the journey.

A common route is via Bantwal and Uppinangady, continuing towards Subramanya. The final route can depend on your pickup location and current road conditions.

⌖
Mangalore → Bantwal → Uppinangady → Kukke Subrahmanya Indicative road route; confirm current navigation before departure.

If you are planning specific sevas or rituals, share the expected timings when enquiring about your taxi. This helps you plan your pickup, temple visit and return journey more realistically.

You can also ask about combining Kukke Subrahmanya with Dharmasthala, subject to your available time and the schedule required for your temple visits.
