Plan Your Bandipur and Nagarahole Journey

Bandipur National Park and Nagarahole National Park are known for forest landscapes and wildlife habitats. A planned taxi journey can help your family or group travel from Mangalore to your selected stay or authorised safari meeting point.

These destinations require advance planning. Check safari availability, entry rules, permitted routes and accommodation arrangements with the relevant authorities or authorised providers. A taxi booking does not itself include park entry or safari tickets.

Share your travel dates, passenger count and destinations so we can discuss suitable transport. Journey duration and road access can vary by route and conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I plan Bandipur and Nagarahole together?

Yes, ask us to discuss a multi-day transport itinerary. The route and timing depend on your accommodation, park access and planned activities.

Can the taxi take us on a safari inside the park?

Do not assume a regular taxi can enter safari areas. Safaris are subject to the park's rules and authorised safari arrangements.

Are safari tickets included in the taxi fare?

Confirm inclusions in writing. Safari tickets, park entry and accommodation should be treated separately unless explicitly confirmed by the relevant provider.

Which vehicle is suitable for a family?

A sedan may suit a smaller group with limited luggage; an Ertiga, Innova, Crysta or Tempo Traveller may suit larger groups, subject to availability.
