Your Journey to Alappuzha

Alappuzha is famous for its backwaters, houseboats, canals and Kerala coastal scenery. A private taxi can help you travel from Mangalore with your family or group and coordinate your preferred arrival time. Since this is a long-distance journey, plan for meal breaks and adequate travel time.

For a quotation, share your travel date, pickup address, passenger count, luggage requirements and whether you need a return journey. Confirm any sightseeing stops and waiting time before booking.

Is Alleppey the same as Alappuzha?

Yes. Alleppey is the commonly used name for Alappuzha in Kerala.

The fare depends on the vehicle, itinerary, trip duration and applicable charges. Contact us for a quotation.

Can I arrange a round trip?

Yes. Provide your return date and any additional stops when requesting the fare.
