Frequently Asked Questions

Mangalore to Bangalore Taxi FAQs

Find answers to common questions before contacting us about your trip.

Can I book a taxi from Mangalore to Bangalore?

Yes. Contact Radhakrishna Taxi Service with your travel date, pickup location, destination and passenger count to enquire about a taxi booking.

Which vehicles can I request for this journey?

You can enquire about sedan cars, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, Tempo Traveller and larger group vehicles. Availability depends on your date and requirements.

Can I arrange a taxi for Bangalore airport?

Contact us with your pickup location, flight schedule and travel date to discuss airport taxi arrangements. Please allow time for us to confirm availability.

How can I enquire about the taxi fare?

Call or WhatsApp us with your travel date, pickup and drop locations, passenger count and preferred vehicle. We can then discuss the applicable fare and trip details.

Can I request a vehicle for a larger group?

You can enquire about a Tempo Traveller or another suitable group vehicle. Please share the number of passengers and luggage requirements so availability can be checked.

What details should I provide when booking?

Please share your travel date, pickup time, pickup location, destination, number of passengers, luggage requirements and preferred vehicle, if you have one.
