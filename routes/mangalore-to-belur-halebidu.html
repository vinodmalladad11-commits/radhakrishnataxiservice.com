A Memorable Temple Journey from Mangalore

Belur and Halebidu are renowned for their Hoysala-era temple architecture, detailed stone carvings and cultural heritage. They are popular destinations for travellers interested in history, architecture and temple visits.

Radhakrishna Taxi Service helps you enquire about private transportation from Mangalore for a planned visit to these historic destinations. You can discuss a trip covering both locations, subject to your schedule and vehicle availability.

Travel duration and the suitable itinerary depend on your pickup point, road conditions, sightseeing time and planned return. Please confirm the itinerary, fare and any additional stops with us before booking.
