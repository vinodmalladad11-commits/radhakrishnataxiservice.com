Mangalore to Sringeri Cab Service

Radhakrishna Taxi Service provides comfortable private taxi service from Mangalore to Sringeri for pilgrims, families, tourists and groups. Sringeri is an important spiritual destination in Karnataka, known for Sri Sharada Peetham, temples and the scenic Tunga River surroundings.

The journey from Mangalore to Sringeri passes through scenic Western Ghats landscapes. A private taxi provides convenient transportation for travellers planning temple visits, family trips and sightseeing in and around Sringeri.
