Travel from Mangalore to Kodaikanal

Kodaikanal is a popular hill station in Tamil Nadu, known for its lake, forested hills, viewpoints and cool-weather escapes. A planned taxi trip can help you manage your travel schedule, luggage and sightseeing stops.

Hill roads and weather may affect travel times. Plan sufficient time for the journey, confirm your accommodation and check local conditions before departure. Contact us with your dates and itinerary for a trip quotation.

Places to Explore

  • Kodaikanal Lake
  • Coaker's Walk
  • Bryant Park
  • Pillar Rocks
  • Upper Lake View and other accessible viewpoints

Access to viewpoints and attractions can change due to weather or local restrictions.
