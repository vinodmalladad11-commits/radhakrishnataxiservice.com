Plan Your Holiday

Suggested Chikkamagaluru Itinerary

This is a starting point for planning, not a fixed tour schedule. Choose the places you want to visit and confirm the practical route with your driver before departure.

Option 1: Short Hill-Stay Trip

  • Depart from Mangalore at an agreed time
  • Travel to Chikkamagaluru and check in at your stay
  • Explore a nearby coffee estate or local attractions
  • Plan hill sightseeing for the following morning
  • Arrange your return journey based on your schedule

Option 2: Hill and Nature Tour

  • Start early for the hill region
  • Plan Mullayanagiri if road access and weather allow
  • Include Baba Budangiri or Dattatreya Peetha
  • Add Manikyadhara Falls if practical
  • Return to your accommodation or continue your planned trip
Some attractions are spread across the district. A full sightseeing circuit may require additional time or an overnight stay. Discuss the route, waiting time, kilometres and applicable charges when requesting your quotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mangalore to Chikkamagaluru Taxi FAQs

How far is Chikkamagaluru from Mangalore?

The road distance is approximately 140–150 km, depending on the route and pickup point. Actual travel time depends on traffic, weather and stops.

How long does the journey take?

Allow roughly 3.5–5 hours as a planning estimate. Ghat roads, roadworks, rain and traffic may increase travel time.

Can I customise the sightseeing itinerary?

Yes, you can enquire about a customised plan. Share the attractions you wish to visit so the route and required time can be discussed before booking.

Which vehicles are available?

Enquire about sedan, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller options. Availability depends on your dates and passenger requirements.

How much does a Mangalore to Chikkamagaluru taxi cost?

The fare depends on the vehicle, trip duration, kilometres, itinerary and applicable charges. Contact us with your travel details for a quotation.

Can I book a taxi for my family?

Yes. Share the number of passengers and luggage details so you can enquire about a suitable vehicle for your family trip.

What details should I send on WhatsApp?

Include your travel date, pickup point, passenger count, preferred vehicle and sightseeing destinations, if any.
