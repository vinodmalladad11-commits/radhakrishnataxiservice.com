Your Journey

From Mangalore's Coast to Coorg's Green Hills

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is popular for its coffee estates, forested hills, waterfalls and scenic viewpoints. Madikeri is a convenient base for exploring several attractions in the region.

A private taxi allows you to plan your departure, make suitable rest stops and discuss sightseeing according to your schedule. It is a practical option for families, couples and groups travelling from Mangalore.

Tell us your destination in Coorg, travel dates, passenger count and preferred vehicle to request a quotation from Radhakrishna Taxi Service.

Suggested Coorg Tour Itineraries

The following plans are examples. The actual route should be adjusted to your hotel location, travel dates and sightseeing priorities.

  • Depart from Mangalore at an agreed time

Frequently Asked Questions

Mangalore to Coorg Taxi FAQs

How far is Coorg from Mangalore?

Madikeri is approximately 135–160 km by road from Mangalore, depending on your exact locations and chosen route.

How long does the journey take?

Allow around 3.5–4.5 hours as a planning estimate for Madikeri. Traffic, ghat-road conditions, rain and stops may increase travel time.

Can I customise my Coorg sightseeing tour?

You can enquire about a customised itinerary. Share the places you want to visit so the route and required time can be discussed before booking.

Which vehicles can I enquire about?

Ask about sedan, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller options, depending on availability and your group size.

How much does a Mangalore to Coorg taxi cost?

The fare depends on the vehicle, total kilometres, trip duration, sightseeing requirements and applicable charges. Contact us for a quotation based on your travel plan.

Can I visit Coorg with my family?

Yes. Share your passenger count, luggage requirements and preferred attractions so you can enquire about a suitable vehicle and itinerary.
