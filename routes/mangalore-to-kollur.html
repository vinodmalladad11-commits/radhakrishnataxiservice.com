Taxi from Mangalore to Kollur Mookambika Temple

Arrange your temple visit with a pickup time and vehicle suited to your travel plans.

Kollur, in Karnataka's Udupi district, is known for Sri Mookambika Temple, an important pilgrimage destination surrounded by the Western Ghats. A private taxi from Mangalore gives your family flexibility to plan the journey around your preferred departure and temple visit.

The usual road journey heads north from Mangalore along NH 66 towards Udupi and Kundapura, before turning inland towards Kollur. The distance and journey time vary with your pickup point, traffic, weather and road conditions. Allow extra time for breaks and temple queues.

Contact us with your travel date, pickup address, passenger count and whether you need a return journey. We can discuss suitable vehicle options and the trip details before you confirm.

Mangalore → Udupi → Kundapura → Kollur Indicative route via NH 66; the final route may vary.

Travel tip: If you are visiting during weekends, festival days or school holidays, consider starting early. Confirm current temple arrangements and local road conditions before departure.

Mangalore to Kollur Taxi – Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Kollur from Mangalore?

Kollur Sri Mookambika Temple is approximately 130–145 km from Mangalore by road. The exact distance depends on your pickup point and route.

How long does the taxi journey take?

Plan for approximately 3–4 hours, depending on traffic, weather, road conditions and breaks.

Can I book a taxi for Mookambika Temple darshan?

Yes. Contact Radhakrishna Taxi Service to enquire about a temple visit and suitable travel arrangements.

Can I arrange a round-trip taxi?

You can enquire about round-trip arrangements. Share your expected temple visit duration and return time so the trip can be planned.

Which vehicles are available for the journey?

Enquire about sedan, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller options. Availability depends on your travel date and passenger requirements.

How do I check the fare?
