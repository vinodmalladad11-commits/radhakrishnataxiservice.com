Mangalore to Ooty Cab Service

Radhakrishna Taxi Service provides comfortable private taxi service from Mangalore to Ooty for families, tourists and groups. Ooty, in the Nilgiris of Tamil Nadu, is known for its cool mountain climate, tea estates, gardens, viewpoints and scenic surroundings.

A private taxi provides convenient transportation for travellers planning a holiday from Mangalore to Ooty. You can plan your journey around your travel schedule and include sightseeing places in and around the Nilgiris.
