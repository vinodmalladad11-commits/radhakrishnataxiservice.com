Your Journey

Travel from Mangalore to the Nilgiri Hills

Ooty is a popular hill station known for its cool climate, gardens, viewpoints and tea-growing landscapes. A private taxi gives you flexibility to plan your departure, rest breaks and arrival according to your travel schedule.

The drive is long and includes hill roads. Allow extra time for traffic, weather, meal stops and any sightseeing planned along the way. Share your itinerary before confirming the booking.
