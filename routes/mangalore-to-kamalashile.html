Travel from Mangalore to Kamalashile

Kamalashile, in the Udupi district region, is a pilgrimage destination known for Sri Brahmi Durgaparameshwari Temple. Travellers can plan a temple visit with family members and discuss convenient pickup and return arrangements.

Temple timings and special-event arrangements may vary. Confirm local details before departure. For a trip quotation, share your travel date, pickup point, passenger count and any additional stops you wish to include.
