Taxi Travel from Mangalore to Jog Falls

Jog Falls is one of Karnataka's best-known natural attractions. The surrounding region offers scenic landscapes and opportunities to plan a relaxed nature-focused trip. A private taxi allows you to arrange your pickup point, breaks and return schedule.
