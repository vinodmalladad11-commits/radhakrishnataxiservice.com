Book a Taxi from Mangalore to Horanadu

Horanadu is renowned for Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple, dedicated to Goddess Annapoorna. Set amid the Western Ghats, it is an important pilgrimage destination for devotees from across Karnataka and beyond.

A private taxi from Mangalore offers a convenient way to travel with your family, plan your departure and arrange suitable stops along the route. The road journey passes through the Malnad region, where road conditions and weather can influence travel time.

Common Questions

Mangalore to Horanadu Taxi FAQs

What is the distance from Mangalore to Horanadu?

Horanadu is approximately 120–126 km from Mangalore by road, depending on your pickup point and route.

How long does the taxi journey take?

Allow around 2.5–3.5 hours as a general estimate. Traffic, ghat road conditions, weather and stops can affect the journey time.

How much does a Mangalore to Horanadu taxi cost?

The fare depends on the vehicle, travel date, trip type, waiting time and additional stops. Contact us for a quotation based on your travel plan.

Can I book a same-day return taxi?

You can enquire about a same-day return arrangement. Confirm your temple visit schedule, waiting time and return pickup before booking.

Can I book a taxi for elderly family members?

You can request a suitable vehicle and discuss pickup arrangements and comfort requirements when booking.

Which vehicles can I request?

Ask about sedan cars, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller options. Availability depends on your travel date and group size.

Can I combine Horanadu with Sringeri or Kalasa?

You can enquire about a combined temple itinerary. Additional destinations affect distance, travel time and fare, so confirm the plan before booking.
