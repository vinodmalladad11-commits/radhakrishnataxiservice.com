12 Best Places to Visit in Coastal Karnataka

Discover beaches, temples, coastal roads, heritage landmarks and scenic stops along Karnataka's Arabian Sea coastline.

Coastal Karnataka is a diverse travel region stretching along the Arabian Sea. It combines pilgrimage towns, long beaches, fishing communities, historic temples, riverside landscapes and small coastal settlements. Starting from Mangalore, travellers can explore the coast northwards or combine selected destinations with an inland journey.

The region is best enjoyed when you plan according to distance rather than trying to visit every attraction at once. A short trip might focus on Udupi and Malpe, while a longer holiday could continue towards Murudeshwar, Gokarna and Karwar.

Southern Coastal Karnataka

1. Mangalore

Mangalore is an ideal starting point, with city temples, beaches, local markets and coastal cuisine. You can spend a day exploring the city or use it as a base for nearby destinations. Read our Mangalore sightseeing guide for more details.

2. Ullal and Someshwara

The southern coast around Ullal and Someshwara offers striking sea views and rocky stretches of shoreline. These areas are better suited to scenic visits than unrestricted water activities. Follow warning signs and stay away from dangerous rocks and strong surf.

3. Udupi

Udupi is well known for Sri Krishna Matha and its traditional food. It also provides access to nearby beaches, making it a flexible destination for pilgrims and families alike.

4. Malpe Beach

Malpe is a popular beach near Udupi. Visitors come for coastal scenery and, when operating conditions allow, boat trips towards St. Mary's Island. Confirm local boat schedules before planning around an island visit.

5. Kaup Lighthouse

Kaup is known for its lighthouse and coastal setting. Visitors should check lighthouse access and operating times in advance. It is often a useful addition to an Udupi-area itinerary when time allows.

Northern Coastal Karnataka

6. Maravanthe Beach

Maravanthe is famous for a scenic stretch where the Arabian Sea and the Souparnika River run on opposite sides of the coastal road. It is one of the region's memorable landscapes. Use safe, designated stopping places rather than stopping in moving traffic.

7. Kundapura

Kundapura provides a gateway to coastal landscapes, local food and nearby temples. It can be a useful overnight stop on a longer journey north, particularly if you want to avoid rushing through several destinations.

8. Murudeshwar

Murudeshwar is recognised for its large Shiva statue, temple complex and sea views. Allow time for the temple area and surrounding attractions. Check opening hours and local conditions before departure.

9. Honnavar

Honnavar offers river, estuary and coastal scenery. The surrounding region includes natural landscapes that appeal to travellers interested in photography and quieter stops. Confirm access and conditions for individual attractions before visiting.

10. Gokarna

Gokarna is both a pilgrimage town and a coastal destination. Visitors can explore religious sites and nearby beaches, taking account of local access rules and the effort required to reach some shoreline areas. It is worth allowing additional time rather than treating every beach as a quick stop.

11. Karwar

Karwar is known for its coastal atmosphere, beaches and views of the Arabian Sea. It can form part of a longer coastal holiday, especially for visitors continuing north after Gokarna. Confirm accommodation and local sightseeing options before setting out.

12. Yana and the Inland Landscape

Although not directly on the coastline, the Yana rock formations offer a contrasting inland excursion for travellers exploring the wider region. This requires additional road time, so it is generally better suited to a longer itinerary rather than a quick coastal day trip.

How to Plan a Coastal Karnataka Road Trip

Two days: Focus on Mangalore, Udupi and selected nearby beaches. Avoid adding far-north destinations.
Three to four days: Consider continuing through Maravanthe and Murudeshwar, with an overnight stop if needed.
Five days or more: Explore farther north towards Gokarna and Karwar, allowing time for sightseeing and rest.

These are broad planning examples, not fixed schedules. The appropriate route depends on your starting point, season, interests and driving conditions.

Best Time and Safety Considerations

Weather can strongly affect beach visits, boat trips and road travel. The monsoon brings heavy rain and rough seas in many coastal areas. During other seasons, heat and humidity can make midday sightseeing tiring. Check forecasts and local advisories, and adjust outdoor activities when conditions are unsuitable.

Respect local customs at temples, protect natural areas and avoid littering. Follow all swimming and boating rules, and use designated parking or viewpoints for photographs.
