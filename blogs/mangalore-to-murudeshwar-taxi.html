Mangalore to Murudeshwar Taxi: Temple and Coastal Trip

Murudeshwar is a memorable destination on Karnataka's coast, famous for its towering Shiva statue, temple complex and Arabian Sea views. Travellers from Mangalore often include it in a pilgrimage, family outing or longer coastal Karnataka tour.

What can you see in Murudeshwar?

The main attractions are Murudeshwar Temple, the large Shiva statue and the surrounding coastal scenery. The temple complex offers striking views of the sea, making it a popular stop for both pilgrims and visitors interested in photography.

Plan your travel schedule

The drive from Mangalore takes several hours, depending on traffic, road conditions and stops. Starting early can provide more time at the destination, particularly if you want to visit the temple and explore nearby places in daylight.

Suggested itinerary

  • Begin your journey from Mangalore at a convenient time.
  • Take a planned refreshment break along the route.
  • Visit Murudeshwar Temple and the Shiva statue area.
  • Allow time for lunch and coastal sightseeing.
  • Return to Mangalore or continue to another pre-arranged destination.

What should you carry?

Carry water, personal essentials, comfortable footwear and suitable clothing for temple visits. Check local temple guidelines and opening hours before departure, especially on festival days.

Book the right vehicle

Consider the number of passengers, luggage and whether you plan to return the same day. For a multi-stop itinerary, confirm the complete route and fare details in advance. Travel times are estimates and may change with traffic or weather.
