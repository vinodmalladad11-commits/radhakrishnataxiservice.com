Mangalore to Coorg Cab

Radhakrishna Taxi Service provides private taxi services from Mangalore to Coorg for families, couples, groups and holiday travellers. You can book a one-way taxi, round-trip taxi or customized Coorg sightseeing trip.

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is a popular hill destination in Karnataka known for coffee plantations, scenic landscapes, waterfalls and Western Ghats attractions. Madikeri is one of the main destinations for travellers visiting Coorg.

A private taxi from Mangalore provides convenient door-to-door travel and allows you to plan stops and sightseeing according to your itinerary.
