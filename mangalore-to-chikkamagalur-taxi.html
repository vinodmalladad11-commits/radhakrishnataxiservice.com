Radhakrishna Taxi Service provides private taxi services from Mangalore to Chikkamagalur for families, couples, groups and holiday travellers. You can book a one-way taxi, round-trip taxi or customized sightseeing trip.

Chikkamagalur, also commonly written as Chikmagalur, is a popular hill destination in Karnataka known for coffee plantations, mountain landscapes, waterfalls and scenic Western Ghats routes.

A private taxi from Mangalore provides convenient door-to-door travel and allows you to plan sightseeing stops according to your itinerary.
